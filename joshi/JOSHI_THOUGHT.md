# JOSHI thought kernel

Status date: 2026-08-16.  
Scope: project constitution, operator phenomenology, research seed, evidence and architecture
contract, and handoff protocol.  
Authority: this document preserves the highest-resolution working understanding reached between
Ember and Codex. It does not turn a hypothesis into a fact, a model into a mechanism, a component
into an integrated system, or a research command into transaction authority.

## Why this document exists

JOSHI is easy to destroy by understanding one attractive fragment too well. A research agent can
study microdips, callouts, chart images, wallet clusters, LP fees, or a presentation overlay; obtain
a clean local result; and silently replace the project with that result. That failure already
happened in the joshibot/Claude history. The consequence was not merely weak code. The estimand was
changed: an adaptive, human–machine, market-wide selection and management process became a small
fixed feature projection, and a null for the projection was narrated as a verdict on the process.

This file exists so that a model exploring one lane can retain the whole object. The canonical
project frame remains [docs/PROJECT.md](docs/PROJECT.md); the broad implementation contract is
[docs/implementation/PROGRAM.md](docs/implementation/PROGRAM.md); the current integration truth is
[docs/implementation/INTEGRATION.md](docs/implementation/INTEGRATION.md); and the microstructure
beacon begins at
[docs/microstructure/trades_quotes_prices/README.md](docs/microstructure/trades_quotes_prices/README.md).
This document joins those records to the operator phenomenology that motivated them. It is
standalone for a model that has not read the originating conversation.

The project uses an explicit epistemic type system:

| Kind | Meaning | What it must not be promoted into |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Observed fact** | Exact bytes, a source occurrence, a finalized chain effect, a captured viewport event, or another witnessed object under a declared boundary | A complete world state, a caused effect, or an identity not contained in the evidence |
| **Implemented capability** | Code and tests can perform a named bounded operation | An integrated path, deployed service, live coverage promise, or economic result |
| **Derived interpretation** | A replayable transformation of facts under a versioned rule and cutoff | Immutable observation or causal truth |
| **Latent estimate or assertion** | A versioned, uncertain explanation such as a wallet cluster, community state, perceived field component, or fitted kernel | A source column, unique identity, or mechanism |
| **Operator perception** | What Ember reported, marked, or acted as though present in a witnessed scene | A machine estimate or objective market fact |
| **Hypothesis** | A falsifiable strategy, measurement, representation, or UI claim | Profitability, safety, or permission |
| **Metaphor** | A coordinate-generating analogy such as pressure, flow, permeability, or topology | A measurement contract or physical law |
| **Aspiration** | The long-horizon operator exocortex and eventual public system | Current scope or current authority |

Whenever prose below moves between these kinds, the transition must be explicit.

## The constitution: what JOSHI is for

JOSHI began as accessibility infrastructure. Existing Pump mobile and browser interactions require
too much painful tapping and precision navigation for Ember's stiff hands, and the website is a
poor fit for Ember's browser workflow. The originating product is therefore a personalized,
keyboard-first, interoperability-centric Pump/Solana cockpit: an assistive client that gives Ember
the broad attention and social surface needed to operate without hand pain. Accessibility is not
a later UI polish lane. It determines the product boundary, the command vocabulary, the need for
large reliable controls, and the refusal to make a narrow exact-mint demo stand in for the system.

The cockpit needs a Pump-like or better information surface: launches, migrations, discovery
boards, live activity, callouts, social/community context, followed-account activity, wallets,
charts, positions, LP state, source health, and the ability to move fluidly among them. Some of
these are public-chain facts; some are Pump application data that is not reconstructible merely by
reading Solana. On-chain transactions, accounts, and program state do not contain Pump's exact
product ranking, personalized following surface, comments, moderation state, viewer-specific
notifications, or the precise bytes and ordering Ember saw. JOSHI must keep those categories
separate while pursuing honest interoperability across both.

The economic and research object is not a standalone predictor. It is Ember's composite
selection–attention–interpretation–execution–management process, coupled to JOSHI's sensing and
presentation and operating inside a market that changes in response to participants:

~~~text
whole observed market
  -> candidates eligible for a product surface
  -> candidates surfaced, ordered, and rendered
  -> Ember's viewport and attention
  -> inspection, comparison, interpretation, and nomination
  -> an armed disposition
  -> entry, abstention, or continued watch
  -> partial exits, full exits, retained exposure, flat watch, and re-entry
  -> eventual resolution, accounting, and retrospective interpretation
  -> changed sensing, presentation, and future operator behavior
~~~

Every arrow is part of the policy and part of the evidence. The system is simultaneously an
accessibility tool, an observational instrument, an elicitation environment, a causal-memory
apparatus, and eventually an advisory exocortex. Its first honest question is not “what model
predicts the next return?” but:

> How does the value of Ember's composite selection–execution–management process change as JOSHI
> improves its sensorium, representations, execution affordances, and degree of automation?

This question permits several answers. Ember's top few choices may contain useful information that
disappears when automated across the market. The value may be risk control, opportunity selection,
reaction time, management, accessibility, or a combination rather than directional prediction.
The strategy may be genuinely negative-EV after full friction and tail loss. No extractable edge
may survive. JOSHI must be able to establish those outcomes without protecting the thesis. What it
must not do is claim to have established them by studying a different process.

The atomic behavioral unit is an **episode**, not a position. One episode can contain several
flat-to-flat inventory epochs, several realized clips, a retained runner, a period during which
Ember remains flat but continues watching, and a later re-entry. A position is an accounting
interval; an episode is continuity of attention, interpretation, and management. Ending inventory
does not necessarily end the hypothesis.

The project also has a longer human context that should be treated respectfully without becoming a
premise in an empirical study. Ember is an expert software engineer with substantial formal
methods, ML, signals-processing, and electrical-engineering background, though not originally a
market-microstructure specialist. JOSHI is meant to turn an accidental foothold in memecoin
economics into durable engineering and research autonomy, more time for human–AI collaboration,
and eventually infrastructure useful beyond this market. Runway pressure is real. It is not
permission to lower epistemic standards, hide losses, rush authority, or narrate a weak proxy into
hope. Ember wants a powerful, reliable foundation worth taking engineering pride in and intends,
after it is useful and de-risked personally, to publish the work under AGPL-3.0-or-later.

## Strategy families are hypotheses, not one bot

The initial strategy families share a sensorium, identity layer, evidence spine, accounting
system, and glass. They must not be collapsed into one policy or treated as profitability claims.
Ember currently estimates that the lived vocabulary contains roughly three to eight dispositions
toward a coin and two to five crackle modes, but those counts are phenomenological leads rather
than a closed enumeration. The system must be able to discover that there are more, fewer, nested,
or continuously varying modes.

### Nominated microdip crackle

Ember selects or nominates a coin using the broad information surface. Automation then watches
reactively for a locally meaningful microdip, obtains current exact-size executable economics, and
supports a very small round trip or another declared management action. The conjecture is that
many tiny **net** profitable crackles could accumulate into a useful bucket and finance
higher-upside experiments. The machine contributes vigilance, latency consistency, quote
discipline, and recordkeeping after a human selection; it must not silently replace nomination
with a crude global screen.

The hypothesis is sensible enough to instrument and dangerous enough to measure harshly. A small
winning move can be overwhelmed by dynamic fees, impact, state movement, failure, MEV, stale
observation, adverse selection, and a single discontinuous loss. “Extremely small PnL” is only a
profit after every landed cost and remaining exposure is included. Linear extrapolation from a
hand-selected handful of successes to thousands of automatic trades is forbidden.

### Partial realization and retained runner

A crackle is an entry and early-management mode, not a coin's destiny. Ember has sometimes entered
for a crackle, recognized some profit, and then perceived that the coin might send. The relevant
action is to realize part of the position while deliberately retaining exposure. RADON, EarthCoin,
and CRASHIUS are requirements examples for that state transition; they are not evidence that the
policy is profitable.

The retained remainder is never “free.” It has remaining quantity, basis, executable liquidation
value, downside, correlation, attention cost, and opportunity cost. The system needs separate
dimensions for entry family, current disposition, thesis, horizon, management act, and review
condition. A runner budget and a distinction between intentional exposure and forgotten dust are
portfolio hygiene, not arguments against preserving convex upside.

### Full exit, flat watch, and re-entry

Ember reports that meaningful PnL can arise from reading a graph, fully exiting, remaining flat
while still attending to the same coin, and re-entering later. This is not an edge case to be
discarded for easier bookkeeping. It is a graph-driven sequence of exposure changes within one
continuing episode. Any model that scores only buy-to-final-sell positions deletes the interval in
which the operator's most economically material decision may occur.

The required state includes exact flatness, continued attention, evolving alternatives, new
evidence, and a fresh basis epoch on re-entry. Counterfactual comparisons must use attainable
quotes at common cutoffs and must not assume that a different exit would leave the later market
path unchanged.

### Fancoin and social transition

The fancoin hypothesis is a distinct longer-horizon book: detect an incomplete transition from an
unofficial reference or fan launch through community formation, represented-person awareness,
claim, participation, endorsement, audience arrival, competition, persistence, fragmentation, or
decay. These are not stages on one inevitable ladder. Fee routing, a permissionless collection
operation, an authenticated social-fee claim, a public mention, sustained participation, and
enthusiastic endorsement are different events.

The estimand is not “do claimed coins go up?” Claims and participation are endogenous and may occur
because the coin is already exploding. The interesting prospective question is whether evidence
available before a transition changes the hazard of a later social transition and whether any
attainable exposure survives selection, liquidity, capacity, and exit costs. Candidate sets must
include dead, duplicated, permanently unclaimed, contested, and misidentified launches.

### LP inventory management

An LP position is an inventory distribution and schedule of contingent trades, not money in a
yield account and not a fee counter. Bin occupancy determines what the position will buy or sell as
price traverses it. The policy question includes whether to add or withdraw capital, how to weight
bins nonuniformly, whether to rebalance in place or close/reopen, which token is being committed,
and whether liquid SOL has better optionality elsewhere.

Continual or state-conditioned volatility-to-bin kernels are plausible research ideas, but the
objective is whole-path economics:

\[
\text{fee and reward income}
-\text{adverse inventory conversion}
-\text{withdrawal and liquidation cost}
-\text{rebalance, composition, network, rent, and route friction}
-\text{risk and opportunity cost}.
\]

Better duty cycle is a controller, not an alpha source. Rebalancing cannot rescue a pool whose
fees fail to compensate for adverse selection and inventory risk.

### Ecology, territory, and watched wallets

Ecology or territory analysis asks whether launches compete to occupy a person, narrative, image,
event, or community and whether attention moves among rival mints. Wallet watching asks whether
accounts Ember deliberately follows or curates can provide early candidate discovery before a
callout. Neither begins as blind copy trading. Both are sensing/context hypotheses: they may help
decide which scope deserves high-resolution collection and human inspection.

“Trend” coins—for example, opportunistic launches around a newly announced entertainment or
cultural event—are one provisional territory family. The family boundary, relevant rivals, start
time, and later winner must be constructed from point-in-time evidence rather than hindsight.

These hypotheses become meaningful only after the system records the market denominator, the
actual followed surface, point-in-time family membership, wallet evidence, Ember's cockpit time,
and failures. A later “winning coin” cannot be backfilled as the canonical member of an earlier
territory.

## The joshibot compost lesson

The old repository is compost: a source of evidence, datasets, scars, audits, design fragments, and
selected reusable organs. It is not the architecture to continue by momentum. See
[docs/reference/JOSHIBOT_COMPOST.md](docs/reference/JOSHIBOT_COMPOST.md).

The recurrent failure was proxy drift. Claude would accept one small description, reduce it to an
oversimplified statistical rule, build substantial code around that rule, and infer too much from
its result. In notation, the real witnessed scene and operator context were \(S_t\), but the study
kept only a lossy projection \(X_t=\pi(S_t)\), tested a crude \(g(X_t)\), and treated a failure of
\(g\circ\pi\) as evidence against Ember's unobserved policy \(f(S_t)\). A negative result for the
projection cannot refute a policy that depends on information the projection discarded. The real
policy may still fail; that study did not identify it.

The amputations were systematic:

| Discarded object | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| The whole candidate universe and actual choice set | Selection ability, platform surfacing, and luck became inseparable |
| Viewport, inspection, comparisons, nomination, and abstention | Human attention was treated as invisible metadata rather than an adaptive sensor |
| Exact order timing, size, caller identity/history, wallet texture, and social context | Distinct latent configurations became one candle pattern |
| Event, availability, render, gesture, send, landing, and finality clocks | Later knowledge and impossible reaction time leaked into studies |
| Exact-size quotes, fees, route capacity, failures, and MEV | Chart motion became imaginary executable PnL |
| Partial exits, residual basis, runners, and correlated remnants | Cash recovery was confused with finished profit |
| Full exit, flat watch, and re-entry | A continuing episode was fragmented into unrelated positions |
| Fresh-mint, curve, graduation, migration, pool, and bin topology | Structural breaks were treated as ordinary stationary returns |
| Social causality and actor-conditioned response | “Callout present” became a Boolean feature |
| LP bin inventory and contingent conversion | Fee accrual became “yield,” with opportunity cost and liquidation omitted |
| Gaps, censoring, changing opportunity sets, and hot-scope selection | Unknown became zero and observed subsets became “the market” |

The old work also demonstrated why green internal tests do not prove the research apparatus.
Replay defects, same-close decision/fill timing, broken fills, lookahead, censoring, incomplete
SELL-path tests, mutation-surviving suites, and hand-selected extrapolations showed that assertion
production outran adversarial verification. JOSHI therefore overbuilds exactness, replay, and
lineage before model sophistication.

There is a positive lesson. The “program synthesis” and “circuit abduction” language Ember used
was not a mistaken product requirement. It was an attempted formalization by a formal-methods and
signals thinker trying to describe an implicit, evolving policy and a partially observed hybrid
system. Later, operator traces may indeed be treated as partial specifications: positive and
negative examples, typed state transitions, temporal constraints, permissible actions,
counterexamples, and missing predicates. Circuit language may help identify state, excitation,
memory, and topology. The mistake was synthesizing before constructing the input language and
measurement apparatus.

“Model-free” in the originating steer did not mean structure-free or anti-mathematical. It meant
that JOSHI should first preserve the market census, attended slice, empirical transitions,
analogous scenes, action paths, and evolving operator vocabulary without declaring one predictive
family to be the market. Structure is introduced where the evidence earns it; the data is not
prematurely compressed to fit the first learning algorithm someone wants to run.

## The field and topology breakthrough

The most productive conceptual breakthrough began with the question: is a market an
incompressible flow over an exotic topology? The disciplined answer is tantalizing but limited:

> A market can be modeled as a conservative transaction network coupled to compressible
> liquidity, inventory, attention, and actor-response fields evolving over an endogenous hybrid
> topology. Price may sometimes be represented as a clearing or dual field. None of this makes the
> market literally an incompressible fluid.

The point of the analogy is to generate typed measurement and operator questions that candles hide.
The governing exact objects remain discrete ledger postings and venue-native transitions. The
field language is admitted only when its domain, measure, unit, clock, observation map, operator,
falsifier, and useful residue are named. The formal research corpus in
[docs/research/field_models/README.md](docs/research/field_models/README.md) should be read as an
anti-physics-theater contract; its
[analogy red team](docs/research/field_models/ANALOGY_REDTEAM.md) and
[study/promotion lanes](docs/research/field_models/JOSHI_LANES.md) are part of that contract.

### Conservative settlement layer

Let a directed account/program/venue graph have incidence operator \(B_t\), atomic balance vector
\(b_t\), oriented transaction flux \(J_t\), and explicit boundary sources and sinks \(s_t\).
At a suitable discrete or coarse time scale,

\[
\dot b_t=-B_tJ_t+s_t.
\]

The sign convention depends on edge orientation. At the exact chain layer this should be a
discrete posting equation, not a numerical derivative. For a closed asset boundary, internal
transfers cancel; mint, burn, fees crossing the declared boundary, wrapping, custody conversion,
and unresolved residuals remain explicit. Conservation is commodity- and boundary-relative. It
does not imply conservation of price, SOL-equivalent wealth, attention, community, liquidity, or
PnL.

This equation also explains why local incompressibility is generally wrong. Even when the global
quantity of a token is conserved, local divergence is exactly how a wallet, vault, pool, or price
bin gains or loses inventory. A divergence-free transaction field would describe only circulation,
such as arbitrage cycles or wash-like loops, while nobody changed exposure. The economically
interesting events usually contain accumulation, depletion, mint/burn, boundary flow, or
conversion.

### Compressible driven fields

For a declared time-varying domain \(X_t\)—price bins, wallets, candidates, graph nodes, or another
typed space—a coarse measure \(\mu_t\) may satisfy a bookkeeping form such as

\[
\partial_t\mu_t+\operatorname{div}_{X_t}J_t
=D_t-C_t+E_t.
\]

Here \(D_t\) is deposition or arrival, \(C_t\) is cancellation, withdrawal, or disappearance, and
\(E_t\) is external forcing, conversion, topology change, or an unresolved term under the chosen
boundary. A richer version may name a separate reaction/conversion operator. The equation predicts
nothing until a constitutive relationship for the fluxes and source terms is specified and
tested.

Liquidity can appear, move, concentrate, and be withdrawn. Attention can arrive through a callout,
ranking change, creator act, or platform-wide event and then decay or fragment. Inventories become
trapped or are released. LPs alter the medium in response to the same activity being modeled.
Participants anticipate other participants. These fields are compressible, stochastic,
self-exciting, history-dependent, reflexive, and observed through selective sensors. “Density per
bin,” “per unit price,” and “per unit log-price” are different measures.

### Price is not literal pressure

The pressure analogy is useful in a precise, limited way. In incompressible Navier–Stokes,
pressure is a dual field enforcing a constraint. In a market-clearing problem, a price can also be
a dual variable or supporting covector that makes incompatible desired asset flows mutually
feasible. On an ideal AMM constraint surface, marginal price is related to the local gradient of
the invariant; for a convex feasible set it can be viewed as a subgradient normal to that set.

But the exact on-chain map is discrete, directional, fee-bearing, integer-rounded, size-dependent,
and capable of refusal. Buy and sell duals differ. Several routes define several feasible sets.
The economically relevant order is often finite rather than infinitesimal. A Pump pool does not
clear all latent social demand; it quotes against deployed state. External arbitrage only glues
venues imperfectly through latency, costs, inventory, and capacity. Thus price is **possibly** a
clearing/dual field under a declared model, not fluid pressure and not a universal sufficient
state.

The chart is best understood as the visible trajectory of a boundary responding to latent and
observed flows, densities, constraints, and forcing. Ember may be solving an inverse problem while
watching it: inferring configurations that could have produced the visible boundary response.
That does not prove the inferred fields are unique or correctly perceived.

### The topology is dynamic, stratified, and hybrid

There is no single Euclidean market space.

- A Pump bonding curve is a profile-bound, integer lattice of valid reserve states with capacity,
  fee, completion, and migration boundaries. A smooth one-dimensional curve is an approximation to
  one core relation, not the full landed state.
- Graduation or migration is a discontinuous topology-changing map. It can change venue,
  coordinates, fees, custody, routes, state dimension, and price object at once.
- PumpSwap has a constant-product-like pricing core but an extended state including raw and
  virtual reserves, fee destinations, canonicality, mint supply, and real payout capacity. The
  ideal invariant is not automatically conserved across the full extended transition.
- Meteora DLMM is a discrete price lattice and porous inventory medium. LPs place separate token
  inventories and shares across bins; active-bin traversal, fixed-width arithmetic, fees, account
  limits, and initialization boundaries matter. A continuous density is optional coarse-graining,
  never the action truth.
- Wallets, funding sources, transactions, bundles, venues, routes, mints, creators, follows,
  callouts, communities, episodes, and scenes form overlapping temporal graphs and hypergraphs.
- Arbitrage glues venue components without making them one venue. New launches, routes, pools,
  communities, and product surfaces add or remove pieces of the state space.

The full state is therefore a bundle of strata rather than one feature vector:

\[
\mathsf S_\omega=
(\mathsf S^{settlement},\mathsf S^{venue},\mathsf S^{route},
\mathsf S^{flow},\mathsf S^{lifecycle},\mathsf S^{wallet},
\mathsf S^{social},\mathsf S^{surface},\mathsf S^{operator},
\mathsf S^{coverage};\mathsf C)_\omega.
\]

\(\omega\) need not be a scalar time. It may name a chain locator, source occurrence, knowledge
cutoff, scene, or event index. \(\mathsf C\) is a set of typed, versioned couplings. An absent LOB
queue is not a zero queue; an absent active bin is not bin zero; an unknown caller identity is not
an anonymous caller with weight zero.

### Transfer limits are part of the model

The formal source is
[docs/microstructure/trades_quotes_prices/TRANSFER_LIMITS.md](docs/microstructure/trades_quotes_prices/TRANSFER_LIMITS.md).
Transfer the question and decomposition before transferring an equation. Queue imbalance,
price-time priority, half-spread economics, LOB depletion races, and queue Fokker–Planck equations
do not directly apply to AMMs. Exact curve traversal replaces queue depletion. A DLMM bin is not a
FIFO queue. A fitted square-root curve never replaces exact Pump or PumpSwap arithmetic. A Hawkes
branching ratio is not the fraction of trades socially caused. A latent-liquidity model is not a
current source fact. A continuum approximation is inadmissible for a decision if exact bins change
that decision.

## Ember's phenomenology: a field bundle, not a pressure score

The breakthrough did not reveal one scalar sensation. Ember explicitly corrected that
interpretation: several partially distinguished factors are considered while hovering over buy or
sell, and **something arises**. The operative state is structured and action-ready, but it is not
necessarily one gestalt and not a conscious weighted sum.

A provisional field bundle is

\[
F_t=\left(
J_t^{orders},
\chi_t^{liquidity},
A_t^{social},
R_t^{actors},
C_t^{chart},
L_t^{lifecycle},
G_t^{PvP},
I_t^{inventory},
\ldots
\right).
\]

The operator-perceived latent configuration is more like

\[
z_t=\Phi\!\left(
F_{\le t},
\text{memory}_t,
\text{portfolio}_t,
\text{choice set}_t,
\text{presentation}_t
\right).
\]

\(z_t\) may contain direction, urgency, timescale, fragility, stored potential, anticipated mode
of release, confidence, and unnamed coordinates. The ellipsis and “unnamed” state are requirements:
the UI and schema must allow Ember to say that something mattered without forcing it into today's
vocabulary. JOSHI must never collapse this into a universal “pressure score.”

The components are related but not interchangeable:

- **Order-flow component:** timing, signed direction, exact size, sequences, splitting, bursts,
  pauses, and whether apparently persistent flow is broad or concentrated in a few actors.
- **Liquidity, susceptibility, and resilience component:** exact-size displacement, directional
  cost, capacity, quote asymmetry, replenishment, withdrawal, absorption, recovery after an
  impulse, route availability, and the rate at which the medium changes under flow.
- **Attention component:** callouts, posts, follows, board rank, viewport arrival, audience
  availability, competing narratives, and the lag between social availability and trading.
- **Actor/caller component:** who or what appears to act, the quality of that attribution, prior
  response history, audience composition, trade-size texture, community tendency, and overlap with
  other callers or clusters.
- **Chart-memory component:** the visible history of prior flow, compression, excursions,
  recoveries, failed breaks, slopes, scales, and the operator's learned visual relations. The
  chart is an observation map, not the state itself.
- **Inventory component:** holder distribution, realized and latent supply, LP deployment, caller
  or fleet inventory, trapped positions, potential forced actions, Ember's exposure, and
  alternative capital needs.
- **Lifecycle/topological component:** fresh mint, curve progress, approach to graduation,
  migration, pool activation, bin edges, route changes, social identity transitions, and coin
  family competition.
- **PvP/churn/compression component:** opposing aggressive flow, rapid turnover, scalper/sniper
  predation, wallet replacement, absorption, recycling, and activity that may generate volume
  without durable displacement.

Identical candle shapes can arise from incompatible configurations. A narrow range may be heavy
opposing flow with replenishing liquidity, rapid turnover among predators, a stable holder base
absorbing sells, or simply nobody trading. A dip may be temporary inventory release within
continuing inflow, the start of caller decay, a liquidity vacuum, a route artifact, or the first
visible boundary response to a lifecycle change. A sharp rise may reflect persistent distributed
demand, one thin-wallet impulse through sparse liquidity, a callout spike about to be sold into, or
arbitrage repricing after migration. Price-only similarity erases the decision-relevant difference.

This reframes the initial strategies without claiming the field picture is true:

- A **microdip** may be a temporary backward displacement while underlying forcing and resilience
  remain favorable. The crackle conjecture becomes: the displacement will relax before the
  relevant forcing dissipates and before friction or tail risk consumes the move.
- A **runner transition** may occur when what looked like a transient impulse begins to resemble a
  sustained source, topology change, or durable actor/attention configuration. This is an operator
  hypothesis, not a model label backfilled from eventual price.
- **Exit and re-entry** may sample successive compression/release or fragility/resilience phases of
  one episode. The analogy helps define what to record; it does not supply the optimal path.

## Response kernels are central

A callout is a marked forcing event, not a Boolean feature. Its mark can include caller identity
or uncertainty, caller/cluster history, content type, coin family, mint age, lifecycle, venue,
territory, overlapping calls, current attention, liquidity geometry, PvP state, exact availability
time, and coverage. The response is multivariate:

~~~text
callout or actor event
  -> wallet-arrival intensity
  -> trade-size and side distribution
  -> signed asset flow
  -> liquidity deployment or withdrawal
  -> exact-size price/quote displacement
  -> decay, recovery, or secondary propagation
  -> community/follow/caller transitions
~~~

For outcome \(Y\), event family \(r\), lag \(\tau\), and pre-event state \(x\), a descriptive
response object may be written

\[
R_{Y\leftarrow r}(\tau\mid x)
=\mathbb E\!\left[
\psi(m_e)\{Y(t_e+\tau)-Y(t_e^-)\}
\mid e\in r,\ X_{t_e^-}=x,\ \text{eligible coverage}
\right].
\]

This is observed conditional response. It is not the caller's caused impact. A caller may
anticipate a move, react to a common source, be surfaced by the same ranking change, or be selected
because the coin was already hot. Overlapping calls, adaptive hot-scope promotion, missing
pre-history, and outcome-conditioned caller reputations can manufacture kernels.

Three objects must remain separate:

1. **Observed response:** what followed the marked event in the realized path.
2. **Prediction:** what a frozen model available at the cutoff forecast conditionally.
3. **Caused or reaction impact:** the counterfactual difference between worlds with and without
   the focal event or action.

Only the first is directly observed. A historical path does not contain both counterfactuals.
Matched controls, discontinuities, natural experiments, instruments, bounds, and sensitivity
analysis may narrow causal claims; they do not make them automatic.

The first useful product is a response-kernel atlas, not a global caller score: conditional
distributions over lag, outcome type, actor/cluster version, context, lifecycle, venue, liquidity,
overlap, and source health, with calibration and support counts. Hawkes and multivariate
point-process models are legitimate forecasting candidates after seasonal and state baselines.
Their cross-kernels do not establish contagion, and near-criticality can be nothing more than a
launch-regime mixture or changing acquisition.

## Wallet, bundle, cluster, and circulation topology

Wallet modeling should use a dynamic multiplex graph or hypergraph. Edge families have different
meaning, evidence, unit, and direction:

- exact funding and asset transfers;
- signed swaps with venue, route, size, and landed effect;
- same-transaction instruction groups and bundles;
- fee payer, relayer, sponsor, signer, and authority relations;
- LP position ownership, adds, removes, claims, and bin inventory;
- follows, callouts, profile-wallet links, and public participation;
- temporal co-trading, common mint arrival, and response similarity; and
- inferred common operation, coordination, caller cohort, or influence.

Exact signed wallet events and decoded account effects can be facts under a named chain closure.
“These wallets coordinate,” “share an operator,” “belong to caller X's cohort,” or “influence one
another” are versioned assertions. Each assertion needs supporting evidence, adversarial
alternatives, confidence or bounds, valid interval, known-at time, model/version, and
supersession/retraction. A router, exchange, common feed, relayer, chance overlap in a small coin
universe, one controller with many wallets, and several coordinated humans can create similar
graphs. A hard cluster ID must not overwrite addresses or historical scenes.

Hyperedges matter. A same-transaction bundle, route with several program legs, multi-account LP
operation, or simultaneous callout cohort is not faithfully represented by unrelated pairwise
edges. Time is also structural: topology changes, identities are learned late, and a relation
valid now may not have been known at the decision cutoff.

Graph Hodge decomposition is a cautious possibility, not a sophistication token. On a fixed,
typed, oriented graph with a declared inner product, one may decompose an edge flow into
gradient/divergent, cyclic/curl, and harmonic components. This could help distinguish net
accumulation from arbitrage-like circulation or flow around structural holes. But the result is
only as meaningful as edge orientation, weights, boundary conditions, identity layers, coverage,
and the interval over which the graph is held fixed. Dynamic nodes, route aliases, missing wallets,
and mixed units can turn “vorticity” into database structure. A Hodge study must beat simpler cycle,
divergence, and participant-concentration summaries and must survive layer ablations before its
components receive market language.

## Human attention is a coupled field and a selection mechanism

Human attention is not metadata attached after a market model. It determines which part of the
market becomes high-resolution data, which candidates are compared, which actions become labels,
and which future streams are promoted. JOSHI must preserve:

- the declared market census and its coverage;
- the eligible product universe;
- candidates surfaced and their exact order;
- actual rendered and viewport sets, not virtual-list overscan;
- opens, focus, dwell, inspection, comparisons, dismissals, and returns;
- nominations, armed dispositions, abstentions, actions, and non-actions;
- the current portfolio and alternatives;
- exact as-known evidence, source health, and cutoff; and
- the sensing and presentation policies that produced the scene.

Viewport presence is not gaze, comprehension, or internal salience. It is still valuable physical
evidence. Likewise, an opened panel is not belief, a hot-scope request is not a completed
subscription, and an abstention is not a prediction of loss.

The observation map is endogenous:

\[
Y_t=O_h(W_{\le t}),\qquad
B_t=R_g(Y_{\le t}),\qquad
(V_t,A_t)=A_{\text{Ember}}(B_{\le t},H_t,I_t,D_t).
\]

Hot-scope acquisition then depends on \(V_t\) and \(A_t\), changing later \(O_h\). High-resolution
missingness is not random. Board rank may be both a consequence of market activity and a cause of
human arrival. Reweighting only the attended sample by an invented propensity cannot recover the
market. Broad cheap census and a cold comparison stratum are necessary to interpret the attended
slice.

## The glass is a versioned intervention

The glass should protect Ember from clerical interruption, not from information. The earlier idea
of “protecting the phenomenon from the UI” was corrected: rich wallet flow, caller history,
response kernels, chart relations, social context, and field representations may be exactly what
helps Ember perceive the market. The problem is not richness. It is unrecorded, unproven, or
inaccessible presentation.

The glass is not a neutral window. It is an intervention in the coupled Ember–JOSHI–market system.
Every presentation is a hypothesis about what is useful to show. The system must record at least:

- **presentation_policy_id**, version, configuration, and digest;
- assignment mechanism and experiment stratum;
- exact content scene bytes and digest;
- full evidence and knowledge cutoff;
- panels, overlays, ordering, grouping, density, and salience;
- eligible items and every surfaced, rendered, initial-viewport, and omitted item;
- typed omission reasons;
- filters, toggles, focus, visibility transitions, and dwell intervals;
- model/field artifacts and uncertainty actually displayed;
- accessibility mode and relevant interaction geometry; and
- operator gestures, annotations, choices, abstentions, latency, and later corrections.

Intended presentation is not actual presentation. A policy definition, assignment, content scene,
render occurrence, viewport event, and operator command are separate artifacts. For a witnessed
choice study, the scene and presentation must be durably acknowledged before reveal; otherwise a
presentation-coverage gap must remain visible.

“What is profitable or helpful to show Ember?” is an estimand with several outcomes:

- net PnL and full liquidation economics;
- tail loss and correlated exposure;
- regret against attainable contemporaneous alternatives;
- decision and execution latency;
- attention cost and task switching;
- overtrading, FOMO, and alert fatigue;
- missed opportunities and coverage displaced by a panel;
- confidence calibration and appropriate abstention;
- accessibility, pain, error rate, and sustained usability; and
- the quality of later operator elicitation.

Presentation science is reflexive. A better display changes Ember's policy and therefore the data
distribution that trained it. A caller overlay can teach labels that later appear predictive. An
alert can create an action and market response. Material UI versions are regime/intervention
boundaries, not harmless cosmetic changes.

Rich layers are welcome when their provenance remains visible:

1. observed source or chain facts;
2. deterministic derived quantities;
3. fitted or abductive inferences with uncertainty;
4. Ember's operator-perceived state; and
5. the presentation intervention itself.

None may quietly become “the pressure.”

## Evidence constitution

JOSHI's durable value depends more on epistemic separation than on any one model. The following
families are not variants of a universal event row:

| Object | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Acquisition** | One request, connection, poll, recovery, browser response, or fixture occurrence, even when equal bytes appeared before |
| **Observation** | Exact retained evidence or an explicitly degraded attestation under a named fidelity boundary |
| **Source event** | A provider- or chain-native occurrence with a complete natural locator; one observation can contain zero to many |
| **Assertion** | A producer/version claim with evidence, validity, known-at order, status, conflict, and supersession |
| **Derived interpretation** | A deterministic or statistical artifact with code/config/input lineage and uncertainty |
| **Latent estimate** | An abductive model output with alternatives, support, identifiability limits, and falsifier |
| **Operator perception** | Ember's scene-bound mark, phrase, gesture, or later correction |
| **Presentation intervention** | What content and representation were actually shown under a versioned policy |
| **Choice set/context** | Honest alternatives, ordering, abstention, and information available at the choice |
| **Command or intention** | An evidence-only semantic act; never a fill merely because its language mentions buying or selling |
| **Landed financial effect** | Finality- and boundary-qualified account changes, independently reconciled |
| **Outcome** | A later measured event or explicit competing-risk/censoring state under a separate cutoff |
| **Policy artifact** | A prospective sensing, ranking, advisory, or controller definition and its support/safety envelope |

### Bitemporal and multi-clock truth

Event-valid time and system-known time must both be retained. A historical join is eligible only
when the version was available by the query's knowledge cutoff, its validity covers the event or
scene, and it is the effective non-superseded branch as known then. A current wallet cluster,
creator field, canonical pool, coin-family winner, or interview label cannot leak backward merely
because it describes an earlier event.

The relevant clock bundle may include chain slot and transaction/instruction order, source event
time, request time, receive time, persist/commit order, availability time, render time, gesture
time, simulation time, send time, landing time, finality, and later enrichment time. These clocks
are partially ordered. Event time does not substitute for wall time; source time does not prove
availability; a block timestamp does not prove same-slot causal order.

### Witnessed replay and retrospective reconstruction

A witnessed scene is the exact view actually delivered: byte-faithful inner view bytes, digest,
mode, scene identity, as-of vector, source/projection watermarks, and evidence cutoff. It may be
replayed exactly after later evidence arrives. A knowledge-cutoff recomputation is a new derived
scene restricted to a historical cutoff. A retrospective scene is another new artifact with a
later outcome cutoff. Neither may overwrite or masquerade as the witnessed bytes.

A screenshot is useful rendering evidence but not the semantic authority. A browser DOM or
normalized JSON object may lose original number lexemes, duplicate keys, ordering, hidden fields,
or fidelity. Exact source response bytes and exact rendered semantic bytes remain separately
identified.

### Identity, coverage, lineage, and protection

Content identity and occurrence identity are different. Equal bytes in two acquisitions can share
one content-addressed blob while retaining two observation occurrences. An occurrence ID is never
inferred from a hash. One source response can contain several market events; one event can be
witnessed by several sources.

Coverage is scoped by source, family, subject, route, cursor kind, and interval. A reconnect does
not close a gap. A successful page does not prove feed completeness. A hot scope does not imply
market-wide coverage. Unknown is not zero; silence becomes zero only when the relevant scope is
healthy and silence has that defined meaning. Cursor authority advances only in the same durable
commit as exact evidence for that scope. Descriptive provider cursors or transport acknowledgments
do not create catalog truth.

Every derived artifact must bind deterministic lineage: input occurrence/assertion IDs, catalog
commit and as-of vector, source gaps, decoder/protocol/model/UI versions, configuration, code or
build identity, output schema, digests, and supersession. Analysis consumes immutable manifested
exports and cannot mutate operational truth.

Protection domain is independent of content hash. Public evidence, authenticated/private Pump
responses, operator notes/scenes, and other private material can contain equal bytes without
sharing physical retention or deletion authority. Private remote spool segments are sealed before
crossing the remote boundary. Secret values never belong in Git, fixtures, logs, process
arguments, browser payloads, agent context, or content-addressed source metadata.

## Accounting and execution semantics

Landed financial truth is independent of intention and interpretation. The ledger starts from
finalized or explicitly provisional before/after account state and derives exact atomic effects.
Classification, episode attribution, and strategy interpretation can remain unknown or be
corrected later without altering what landed. Internal controlled-wallet transfers cancel at the
declared household boundary; external wallet changes and unexplained residuals remain visible.

The system must separate:

- intention, command, constructed transaction, simulation, signature, send, landing, failure,
  finality, and reconciled effect;
- order, transaction, instruction, economic fill, wallet effect, lot, inventory epoch, management
  tranche, and operator episode;
- gross proceeds, all fee/rent/tip/transfer components, net realized cash, exact basis, realized
  PnL, remaining quantity and basis, and current executable exposure;
- a partial exit from a thesis/disposition transition;
- capital recovery from profit;
- current mark, marginal pool price, intended-size quote, actual average fill, full-position
  liquidation, and stressed liquidation;
- time exposed from time watching flat; and
- action observed outside JOSHI from proof that JOSHI initiated it.

Basis carries provenance and quality: known, estimated, partial, or unknown. Unknown basis is not
zero and not current mark. Disposal uses explicit lot slices; no hidden FIFO/LIFO rule becomes
fact. A partial disposal allocates exact rational basis and leaves the runner economically live.
Exact flat closes an inventory epoch without necessarily closing the episode. Re-entry begins a
new basis epoch.

Executable values require size, direction, asset pair, exact protocol state, fee profile, route,
observation/expiry, and refusal semantics. Marks have no capacity claim. A quote is not a fill.
Full liquidation can be non-additive across holdings because combined actions consume shared
liquidity and change state.

All strategy evaluation includes protocol/LP/creator/transfer fees, priority fees and tips,
rent/account effects, price impact, slippage bounds, quote age, landing delay, route failure,
unlanded attempts, adverse selection, MEV/sandwich/backrun risk, residual inventory, opportunity
cost, and correlated tail states. Apparatus error must be materially below the target edge. If it
is not, the study can conclude only that the edge is not measurable at that scale.

There is **no current transaction authority**. Current operator commands are evidence-only and
observe-only. Pure quote and liquidity math cannot build instructions. Any later signer must be a
separately authorized, small capability process with independent guards and reconciliation.

## LP semantics in full

For a DLMM position \(i\), a useful exact representation is a discrete inventory measure over bins
and assets:

\[
\mu_i^{LP}=
\sum_j\left(
x_{i,j}\delta_{(j,X)}+
y_{i,j}\delta_{(j,Y)}
\right),
\]

with per-bin price, pool assets, liquidity supply, position share, fee/reward checkpoints,
initialized state, active bin, profile, token programs, and observation closure. Principal,
pending fees, and pending rewards are distinct. Missing accrual data is unsupported/unknown, not
zero.

The operator needs:

- exact token composition now and under withdrawal;
- per-bin shares, weights, and contingent buy/sell orientation;
- price geometry and active-bin relation;
- fees and rewards by asset and evidence quality;
- basis and custody provenance;
- adds and partial/full removes;
- claims with independent evidence;
- in-place rebalance versus close/reopen with position identity preserved or changed accordingly;
- withdrawal inventory and separate leg-liquidation quotes;
- transaction, composition, rent, account, chunking, slippage, and route costs;
- counterfactual hold/no-change paths;
- capital left free for other opportunities; and
- whole-path opportunity cost.

Withdrawal returns the assets the position currently contains. It does not magically restore SOL
already converted into another token. An allocator may rationally prohibit new SOL deposit, remove
only some bins, keep a token-only ask schedule, reserve liquid SOL, or do nothing.

Protocol capability, SDK helper, modeled math, deployed instruction support, and website UI
control are different claims. JOSHI must not say a control exists because an ideal policy needs it
or an SDK seems to expose a similarly named operation. Exact deployed-profile parity requires raw
accounts, official comparator behavior where available, read-only simulation, fixed finalized
vectors, affected-account closure, and UI parity if a UI claim is made. Until then, a rebalance
projection is modeled only and names its unsupported fields.

## Acquisition shape and access posture

JOSHI needs two coupled acquisition tiers.

**Broad cheap census** preserves the denominator: launches, migrations, selected product boards,
coarse market activity, source coverage, and other low-cost signals sufficient to know what
existed and what could have been selected.

**Promoted or leased hot scopes** collect high-resolution trades, reserve/account state, exact
quotes, watched wallets/cohorts, chart evidence, social activity, and route state for candidates
Ember or a later sensing policy nominates. Each lease has a reason, scope, TTL, capacity cost, and
desired/applied/acknowledged state. Promotion is part of the observation policy and must be logged.

The intended primary Pump product path is a headless, read-only, honestly authenticated adapter
that reproduces the ordinary information purposefully delivered to Ember's session under reviewed
route, rate, retention, and access constraints. The browser companion is reconnaissance,
direct-versus-official parity, schema/auth drift detection, and accessibility fallback. It is not
the desired continuous production pipeline, because Ember's laptop/browser hangs, sleeps, loses
power, and cannot be the availability boundary.

This access posture does not claim that all Pump application data is public-chain data or that a
technical response settles rights. It distinguishes:

- public Solana/program state acquired through public RPC or Helius;
- licensed/provider data under the actual Helius and PumpPortal account boundaries;
- officially described Pump read routes;
- observed but undocumented Pump product routes;
- authenticated user-session routes purposefully delivered to Ember; and
- reconnaissance-only surfaces whose authentication, replay, privacy, completeness, or stability
  remains unresolved.

No fraud, authentication bypass, key mining, identity rotation, wallet challenge automation,
forged browser identity, or security testing belongs in the acquisition plan. The direct adapter
stops on authentication rejection, persistent rate limits, device binding, a need for wallet
signing, or a route outside its audited catalog. Current repository docs provide an engineering
access posture for a personal tool, not settled legal conclusions. See
[docs/implementation/lanes/10_direct_pump_api.md](docs/implementation/lanes/10_direct_pump_api.md).

Helius, PumpPortal, and public Solana acquisition are separate source contracts with exact quotas,
prices, commitments, gaps, and evidence fidelity. A provider credential is not a wallet key.
PumpPortal's data/trading-wallet capability boundary must be treated conservatively; paid events
are not enabled merely because a key exists. Source credentials are supplied by safe path to the
smallest acquisition process. Wallet private keys never belong in collectors, the glass, the
store, analysis, remote spool, or remote hosts.

Continuous acquisition should write bounded append-only segments, separated by protection domain,
with exact occurrence and byte closures. Private segments are authenticated-encrypted before
replication. Remote ACK means exact ciphertext durability at a named replica generation; it does
not advance a source cursor, create a catalog commit, close a gap, or authorize deletion. The Mac
remains the sole catalog-admission writer in the current plan, receiving resumable segments and
building local SQLite/CAS/exports.

The dated host finding matters. [docs/implementation/REMOTE_TOPOLOGY.md](docs/implementation/REMOTE_TOPOLOGY.md)
records that neither persvati nor hbox is currently approved for deployment: both run end-of-life
Ubuntu releases. Persvati is the better no-new-cost collector candidate after a supported-OS
upgrade, dedicated service identity, storage/encryption decision, and lid/power/reboot continuity
canary. Hbox is better treated as a ciphertext replica or later batch worker after its memory/swap
gate and explicit acceptance of the nonredundant ZFS special-vdev risk; it is never the sole copy.
Tailscale reachability was asymmetric and requires a bidirectional canary. Hetzner is an optional
later supported-LTS collector/off-site failure domain after a measured need and explicit purchase
authorization. No remote host receives wallet signing material.

Graceful degradation protects the denominator: shed media/enrichment first, slow social polling,
reduce hot-scope breadth/TTL, retain launch/migration census if possible, and stop cleanly before
resource exhaustion. Every scope reduction, overflow, corrupt segment, downtime interval, and
unrecoverable live-only loss becomes evidence.

## The learning agenda

JOSHI must learn in an order that preserves the question.

### 1. Record, replay, and measure the apparatus

First make exact acquisition, coverage, point-in-time joins, scenes, presentation, gestures,
commands, landed accounting, quotes, episodes, and immutable exports reliable. Build synthetic
known-truth traces that contain duplicates, gaps, late correction, topology changes, same-shaped
charts from different fields, partial exits, flat watch, re-entry, route loss, and deliberate
causal confounding. If the apparatus cannot recover or refuse those truths, it is not ready for
market inference.

### 2. Use sparse open operator marks

During live decisions, do not demand a questionnaire. One keystroke, semantic chart anchor, short
phrase, gesture, or voice fragment should freeze the scene. Allow provisional disposition,
crackle family, direction, urgency, timescale, compression, absorption, fragility, “another thing
I cannot name,” and “nothing articulable.” The taxonomy stays open.

Chart drawing and region/trajectory gestures—whether native to JOSHI or learned from a
Padre-like surface—may be a particularly direct elicitation language. Retain the gesture geometry,
chart transform, visible time/price domain, scene digest, tool semantics, and resulting annotation;
do not reduce a drawn relation to one label before learning what distinction Ember was making.

After the episode, replay the actual witnessed scene and conduct a richer interview. Immediate
phenomenology and retrospective interpretation are different artifacts. The interview must ask
whether Ember is recovering the original perception or constructing a cleaner story after seeing
the outcome. Later language never overwrites the original mark.

### 3. Establish descriptive and analog baselines

Build point-in-time datasets with full choice denominators and explicit gaps. Start with:

- exact venue price/quote/fill/liquidation calibration;
- market census and attention-funnel summaries;
- signed flow, wallet concentration, liquidity concentration, and lifecycle-stratified
  descriptive fields;
- nearest-neighbor or multimodal analog retrieval with an explicit “none analogous” result;
- retrieval evaluated partly by whether Ember agrees that scenes feel structurally similar; and
- survival tables, competing risks, and episode paths without strategy claims.

Analog retrieval is valuable before a high-capacity predictor because it exposes representation
failures and gives Ember something inspectable.

### 4. Fit separate forecasting and inference problems

Candidate families include:

- marked point processes for order, wallet, callout, creator, attention, liquidity, and topology
  events;
- seasonal/negative-binomial/state baselines before Hawkes;
- caller-/cluster-/context-conditioned response kernels;
- survival and competing-risk models for graduation, route loss, community transition, collapse,
  exit, runner promotion, and re-entry;
- dynamic graph and hypergraph summaries, temporal embeddings, cycle/divergence/Hodge tests, and
  topology-change models;
- state-space or abductive models for operator-perceived configurations;
- multimodal chart/order/social representations;
- conditional outcome models for attainable actions;
- choice, preference, abstention, and uplift models over the actual choice set; and
- calibrated ensembles whose members retain separate support, clocks, and uncertainty.

Hawkes is a forecasting candidate, not the ontology. Graph neural networks do not turn inferred
edges into facts. Image models do not make candles sufficient state. LLM text interpretations are
versioned assertions with prompts, inputs, model identity, time, and uncertainty.

### 5. Learn sensing and presentation before automatic trading

The first policy-learning problem should be active sensing:

- which candidate deserves a hot scope;
- which wallet/cohort or social thread deserves acquisition budget;
- which exact state or quote would most reduce decision uncertainty;
- which historical analog is worth retrieving;
- which panel, overlay, order, and salience help Ember now; and
- when silence is preferable to an alert.

This is still policy and therefore needs prospective versions, support bounds, randomization or
matched controls where safe, resource costs, and UI-intervention logging. It can create value
without transaction authority.

### 6. Only later consider advisory or execution policy

An advisory model must join calibrated field/state inference to attainable action alternatives,
full accounting, tail risk, capacity, and Ember's current portfolio. Automation can expand only
after the value-dilution curve from Ember's top choices toward broader machine scope is measured.
Any future execution policy requires independent capability safety, prospective declaration,
simulation and landing parity, complete path scoring, and immediate stop conditions.

The long-horizon aspiration includes neural operator and learned constitutive-field models. A model
might learn how flow, liquidity, attention, actor topology, lifecycle, and presentation evolve or
how Ember's perception complements machine sensing. The aspiration is human–machine
complementarity: reconstruct parts of Ember's perceived configuration, expose machine-observed
components Ember missed, and learn where each is calibrated. It is never simply
“features \(\rightarrow\) next return.”

### Core empirical hazards

Every study must address:

- reflexivity and policy-version drift;
- endogeneity of calls, rank, attention, and operator selection;
- lookahead through source time, current identity, later outcomes, embeddings, and interviews;
- missing-not-at-random high-resolution data from hot scopes;
- source outages correlated with extreme activity;
- survivorship, left truncation, right censoring, competing risks, and route disappearance;
- changing launch, fee, protocol, liquidity, social, and product regimes;
- multiple testing and researcher degrees of freedom;
- capacity, correlated exposure, scale dilution, and market impact;
- Solana ordering, failed attempts, priority/tip economics, and MEV;
- model/UI feedback into future labels and actions; and
- the possibility that gross structure exists but no extractable edge survives friction.

## The microstructure beacon

The formalized Bouchaud–Bonart–Donier–Gould corpus is a guiding beacon because Ember recognized in
it a formal vocabulary near the folk microstructure theory developed while staring at Pump charts.
Its value is not a bag of LOB equations. It teaches JOSHI to ask better transfer questions.

The core lessons, elaborated in
[docs/microstructure/trades_quotes_prices/JOSHI_BEACON.md](docs/microstructure/trades_quotes_prices/JOSHI_BEACON.md),
are:

- price, signed flow, liquidity, state, and execution are coupled;
- nearly unpredictable returns can coexist with predictable flow and structured adaptation;
- event time and wall time answer different questions;
- observed response is prediction plus reaction and does not identify caused impact;
- response is not the bare propagator kernel when order flow is correlated;
- liquidity can adapt asymmetrically to expected and surprising flow;
- provider revenue is fee/spread capture minus selection, inventory, liquidation, priority, and
  operating cost;
- an instantaneous mark is unsafe for a large or illiquid position;
- venue-native exact state and size-specific quote/fill geometry precede predictive work; and
- a simple baseline is useful primarily because its failure reveals missing structure.

Two prohibitions follow. **Phantom replay** inserts or removes a historical trade while holding
later market, social, route, and MEV state fixed, then calls the result a counterfactual. It is
forbidden except as an explicitly named frozen-path mechanical calculation. **Causal theater**
fits an event kernel or observes a post-event rally and labels it impact, contagion, or influence.
It is forbidden without an identification design and honest bounds.

The model dependency map in
[docs/microstructure/trades_quotes_prices/FORMAL_MODEL.md](docs/microstructure/trades_quotes_prices/FORMAL_MODEL.md)
and open lanes in
[docs/microstructure/trades_quotes_prices/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](docs/microstructure/trades_quotes_prices/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)
are research inputs, not strategy recommendations.

## Engineering posture

The foundation should be broad in capability but narrow in semantic authority.

### Stable core and data plane

- Rust is the modular core for exact contracts, evidence admission, one-writer storage,
  acquisition, accounting, protocol math, liquidity semantics, replay, projections, and spool
  durability.
- React/TypeScript is the accessibility-first glass with strict runtime contracts, exact
  scene/presentation/command identity, keyboard operation, and no financial arithmetic authority.
- SQLite is the single operational truth writer, with forward migrations, full commit ordering,
  WAL/FULL durability, explicit writer lease, immutable observations/assertions/scenes/commands,
  and typed bitemporal queries.
- Exact bytes live inline or in content-addressed blobs under protection-domain policy. Content
  hash does not replace occurrence identity.
- Research receives immutable, registered, manifested Parquet snapshots. DuckDB and pinned Python
  are an ephemeral analysis workbench, not a second operational database.
- Exact integers, decimal-string wire values, reduced rationals, microsecond UTC, monotonic clock
  domains, algorithm-qualified hashes, closed schemas, duplicate-key rejection, and explicit
  state/refusal types are non-negotiable at boundaries.
- Schema evolution, supersession, correction, deterministic lineage, offline fixtures, crash
  matrices, byte goldens, property tests, differential protocol fixtures, and replay after reopen
  are part of the product.

### Reuse and bespoke boundary

Reuse mature crates, official protocol documentation and SDKs as quarantined comparators, Solana
read clients, HTTP/WebSocket libraries, SQLite, Arrow/Parquet, DuckDB, charting primitives, and
commodity execution/routing rails where their custody and semantic boundaries are proven. Do not
write an event broker, RPC stack, dataframe engine, or strategy framework for identity.

The bespoke work is the reason JOSHI exists:

- the Pump-like accessibility surface and operator glass;
- exact evidence/coverage/known-at ontology;
- the market-wide census to attended-slice linkage;
- scene, presentation, choice-set, gesture, and interview capture;
- wallet/social/lifecycle identity with versioned uncertainty;
- episode, runner, flat-watch, re-entry, and accounting semantics;
- LP contingent-inventory truth;
- operator-perceived field configurations and response-kernel research; and
- the human–machine complementarity loop.

### What is worth overbuilding

Overbuild only foundations that compound across every later project:

- exactness and unit safety;
- occurrence and content identity;
- provenance, bitemporality, coverage, and replay;
- lineage and immutable exports;
- privacy/protection domains and capability separation;
- schema evolution, correction, and supersession;
- deterministic offline operation;
- crash and adversarial testing; and
- accessible, faithful operator capture.

Do not overbuild premature distributed infrastructure, a generic strategy DSL, cross-language
schema empires, online feature stores, model registries, GPU orchestration, autonomous controllers,
or a large signer/execution stack before a measured consumer exists. A remote append-only spool is
earned by continuity needs; it is not permission for distributed catalog authority.

The source tree is AGPL-3.0-or-later. Dependency license/provenance and publishable fixtures are
part of the foundation.

5. **Store export to analysis:** generate real Parquet from a store cutoff, reconcile every
   manifest/part/row/coverage/lineage field, register atomically, and bind deterministic analysis
   runs to that snapshot.
6. **One offline root gate:** implement the common fixture and root readiness command that walks
   exact source bytes through store, witnessed scene, semantic mark, export, analysis, reopen, and
   digest reproduction under network and credential denial.

The direct Pump API has only a bounded officially described public smoke. One authenticated route
still needs the Ember-present parity protocol before promotion. Pump information parity is not
currently claimed. Continuous remote acquisition is not deployed. Both candidate local remote
hosts remain blocked by the documented OS and host gates. No current process constructs, signs,
submits, rebroadcasts, or authorizes a transaction.

## Anti-reduction rules

These rules are constitutional. A future model may disagree with a hypothesis; it may not silently
change the object.

1. Do not call JOSHI a predictor. Name the specific sensing, inference, presentation, choice,
   execution, or management problem.
2. Do not use a tiny exact-mint or selected-trade study to pronounce on the complete Pump-like
   operator process.
3. Do not treat a candle, current rank, callout flag, wallet count, or “pressure” score as market
   state.
4. Do not omit the observed universe, surfaced/viewport/inspected/armed denominators, or
   abstentions when studying selection.
5. Do not end an episode at flat inventory when attention and re-entry remain active.
6. Do not call recovered capital free exposure or mark-times-balance liquidation value.
7. Do not call LP fees profit or LP position value without exact inventory, withdrawal,
   liquidation, friction, and opportunity cost.
8. Do not treat observed response as caused impact, a Hawkes kernel as contagion, or a cluster as a
   person.
9. Do not apply LOB equations to AMMs before defining the venue-native analogue and transfer
   limits.
10. Do not convert unknown, unsupported, censored, quarantined, or uncovered data to zero.
11. Do not use later creator, cluster, territory, canonical-pool, outcome, or interview knowledge
    in an as-known scene or feature.
12. Do not insert a hypothetical historical action while freezing its adaptive future and call the
    result policy PnL.
13. Do not treat a passing component test as an integrated path, a modeled operation as deployed
    support, or an SDK method as UI/protocol parity.
14. Do not protect Ember from rich information; protect Ember from clerical interruption,
    provenance loss, inaccessible controls, and unmeasured persuasion.
15. Do not let financial urgency become a prior that edge exists. Do not let appropriate caution
    become an excuse to amputate the ambitious sensorium.
16. Preserve the useful instrument when a model fails. A failed crackle rule may leave quote
    calibration; a failed field model may leave flow glass; a failed caller model may leave an
    exact response atlas.

## Open research directions

The following are lane seeds, not a backlog to execute indiscriminately.

### Constitutive operators

Can a sparse, state-dependent operator map signed marked flow, exact liquidity geometry, actor
context, lifecycle, and attention history to short-horizon quote/liquidity evolution better than
seasonal, exact-protocol, and simple autoregressive baselines? Which restrictions—linearity,
stationarity, additivity, fixed topology—fail first? The target should be a vector of typed
outcomes, not one price.

### Field/state measurement maps

What observation maps from raw transactions, exact pool state, social events, product surfaces,
and chart pixels preserve the distinctions Ember uses? Which coordinates are stable across
rendering and venue changes? Can same-shaped charts with known different latent generators be
distinguished prospectively? What unnamed operator labels remain irreducible to current sensors?

### Operator-perceived-state inference

Given scene-bound sparse marks and later interviews, infer a distribution over \(z_t\), not a
single label. Separate prediction of Ember's mark, reconstruction of the evidence that supports
it, and prediction of market outcomes conditional on it. Test whether the machine can identify a
field component Ember omitted and whether Ember provides information absent from machine sensors.

### Response-kernel atlas

Estimate caller-, wallet-cluster-, context-, lifecycle-, territory-, and venue-conditioned
responses for wallet arrival, trade size, signed flow, liquidity, quote displacement, decay, and
secondary propagation. Preserve overlapping events and common-cause baselines. Compare event-time
and wall-time kernels, and report support/calibration rather than a caller leaderboard.

### Caller, wallet, bundle, and cluster models

Develop bitemporal multiplex and hypergraph representations with relation-specific evidence.
Estimate coordination or common-operation equivalence classes under adversarial alternatives.
Measure how predictive conclusions change when uncertain edges are removed, delayed to their
known-at time, or replaced by router/common-feed explanations.

### Topology transitions

Build exact launch/curve/completion/migration/pool/route splice artifacts. Measure asset and state
pushforward residuals, price-object discontinuity, attention continuity, and episode continuity.
Study whether pre-transition flow/attention predicts transition hazard without using the eventual
pool or success label early.

### Compression taxonomy

Elicit and operationalize multiple compressions: opposing heavy flow, absorption, replenishing
liquidity, PvP turnover, thin silence, holder concentration, quote-capacity contraction, attention
crowding, and pre-migration boundary effects. Construct synthetic and real contrast sets with
similar candles. Falsify any scalar compression score that cannot distinguish their outcomes or
operator meanings.

### Hodge and circulation tests

On fixed, typed wallet–venue–asset graphs, compare simple net flow, cycle enumeration, divergence,
and full Hodge components. Ask whether cyclic/harmonic flow adds held-out information about
arbitrage, churn, or false activity after route and actor controls. Reject interpretations unstable
to graph boundary or edge-layer choice.

### Attention-flow inference

Model census-to-surface-to-viewport-to-inspection-to-arm transitions jointly with market and
product state. Estimate incremental value at each funnel stage and the scale-dilution curve from
Ember's choices to machine-expanded scope. Treat platform rank and hot-scope promotion as
endogenous. Preserve a cold-market stratum.

### Presentation-policy science

Version panels, overlays, ordering, salience, explanations, density, and alert policies. Study
helpfulness across PnL, tails, latency, attention, regret, calibration, accessibility, and
overtrading. Use safe randomization of presentation—not random capital deployment—where possible.
Measure learning and carryover between UI versions.

### Active sensing and promotion

Choose which mint, wallet, callout, route, pool account, or social thread to acquire at higher
resolution under budget and coverage constraints. Optimize information gain or decision value
while retaining the census denominator. Study whether the sensing policy creates blind spots that
look like market structure.

### LP permeability and inventory control

Treat exact DLMM bins as a controllable discrete medium. Estimate occupancy, fee-bearing flow,
toxicity, stranding time, and conversion risk by relative bin and state. Compare no change,
withdraw only, one-sided redeployment, nonconstant weights, in-place rebalance where proven, and
close/reopen. Include alternative capital use and full leg liquidation.

### Crackle feasibility

Start at Ember's nomination, not an outcome-selected pattern. Measure attainable dip entry,
current round-trip hurdle, delay, quote/fill error, exit ladder, intervention, and tail loss.
Separate selection value, wait-for-dip value, management value, and execution value. Stop if
measurement error or correlated tails dominate the gross edge.

### Runner and episode management

Compare prospectively declared partial/full/runner/flat-watch/re-entry paths at common cutoffs and
terminal horizons. Preserve residual basis, liquidation, risk, opportunity set, and attention.
Study whether disposition transitions can be recognized before the outcome and whether apparent
runner upside survives cemetery and concentration effects.

### Synthetic known-truth falsifiers

Generate hybrid jump systems with exact venue mechanics, known latent regimes, controlled wallet
fleets, callout confounds, ranking feedback, adaptive hot scopes, topology changes, MEV ordering,
gaps, and operator labels. Determine which inference pipelines recover the known structure and
which confidently hallucinate pressure, causation, or identity.

### Causal bounds

For callouts, presentation changes, operator actions, and LP edits, specify what is point
identified, bounded, or unidentified. Use negative controls, matched scene alternatives, natural
experiments, staggered availability, sensitivity sets, and explicit interference. Prefer useful
bounds to decorative causal coefficients.

### Calibrated model ensembles

Combine exact protocol calculators, simple statistical baselines, point processes, graph models,
chart/multimodal encoders, survival models, and operator-perception models without erasing their
support or authority. Calibrate by lifecycle and regime. Ask when model disagreement should trigger
more sensing, a richer display, abstention, or an operator interview.

## Instructions to an exploring model

Before doing any JOSHI exploration, write a compact study preamble containing all ten items:

1. **Object:** which part of the composite Ember–JOSHI–market process is being studied?
2. **Estimand:** what exact contrast, forecast, calibration, description, or falsification is
   sought?
3. **Observed universe:** what market census, candidate denominator, choice set, wallet/social
   scope, venue, lifecycle, and coverage are represented?
4. **Clock and cutoff:** what event/order clock is used, what was known and rendered by the decision
   cutoff, and what later outcome cutoff is allowed?
5. **Acquisition requirements:** which exact source bytes, chain accounts/effects, product
   surfaces, quotes, scenes, presentations, and gaps are required?
6. **Confounds and identifiability:** what selection, endogeneity, common causes, MEV, censoring,
   topology change, actor ambiguity, and observation-policy effects remain?
7. **Falsifiers:** what result would reject the hypothesis, the transfer, the measurement map, or
   the apparatus itself?
8. **Transfer limits:** which source-book, physics, graph, venue, or causal analogy is used, and
   where does it stop?
9. **UI implication:** what would be shown to Ember, how would that presentation change the coupled
   policy, and what provenance/uncertainty must remain visible?
10. **Smallest informative prospective study:** what bounded, point-in-time study can discriminate
    among live alternatives without pretending to answer the whole project?

Then follow this protocol:

- Read this document, [docs/PROJECT.md](docs/PROJECT.md), the current
  [integration review](docs/implementation/INTEGRATION.md), and the relevant canonical lane before
  proposing code.
- State epistemic kinds for every input and output. Keep observed, derived, inferred,
  operator-perceived, presented, intended, landed, and outcome objects distinct.
- Prefer an offline fixture, a synthetic known-truth adversary, and a prospective capture contract
  before a retrospective market claim.
- Compare against the simplest defensible exact or statistical baseline. Include negative controls
  and chronological holdout.
- Report measurement error, support, coverage, uncertainty, costs, tails, capacity, and useful
  residue.
- If the data cannot answer the question, say which new sensing or scene evidence would make it
  answerable.
- Never run a tiny amputated proxy study and pronounce on JOSHI, Ember's intuition, “memecoin
  alpha,” or the whole market.
