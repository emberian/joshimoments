# Wave 5 — the living instrument

Status: primary Wave 5 execution plan, replanned 2026-08-17 after the Wave 4
`useful_partial` handoff. This plan grants no trading, liquidity-deployment,
transaction-construction, signing, submission, or autonomous economic authority.

The detailed learning program in
[`WAVE5_LEARNING_FIELD_LAB.md`](WAVE5_LEARNING_FIELD_LAB.md) remains a research
specification beneath this plan. Its estimator order, artifact contracts, baseline ladder,
falsifiers, compute budgets, routed-liquidity envelope, and transfer limits remain useful. Its
old execution order, universal `W5.A` gate, and single definition of “Wave 5 complete” do not.

## 1. Executive decision

Wave 5 makes JOSHI a **living instrument** before it asks JOSHI to become a successful learning
system.

The first product is a broad, accessible, read-only market sensorium with a faithful scientific
memory:

```text
broad declared market surface
  -> cheap census and explicit gaps
  -> bounded, reasoned hot observation
  -> exact evidence/admission waist
  -> immutable cockpit publication
  -> accessible daily Glass
  -> scene, disposition, episode, and outcome memory
  -> reproducible research cut
  -> earlier-only retrieval
```

The epistemic position book begins early, beside that memory:

```text
truthful witnessed scene
  -> preregistered bounded claim
  -> sealed Ember/base-rate/simple-model submissions
  -> later adjudication or typed censoring
  -> retrospective scorecard
  -> only much later: ensemble, information allocation, or shadow policy
```

Forecast skill is not the entrance fee for product usefulness. Exact quote/fill/liquidation
calibration is a per-venue and per-profile capability, not a universal gate on the cockpit.
Routed liquidity, advanced field models, Hawkes processes, multimodal ensembles, and coupled
controllers remain alive as an incubator; none is permitted to manufacture the first success
witness.

The Wave 4 verdict remains historical fact:
[`useful_partial`](../implementation/wave4/00_INTEGRATION_HANDOFF.md). Wave 5 closes its open
operational joins under new witnesses; it does not rewrite Wave 4 as complete.

## 2. Commitments fixed by this replan

1. **Daily usefulness is the primary product criterion.** JOSHI should become a surface Ember
   voluntarily reopens because it supports real observation and position-management tasks with
   less navigation or physical burden. Time open, clicks, trade count, and PnL are not success
   metrics.
2. **The information surface is broad before it is deep.** Launches, discovery and boards,
   callouts/follows, watched wallets, lifecycle, pools/routes, social/community state, and
   position context remain first-class. One excellent exact-mint corridor cannot qualify breadth.
3. **The architecture has a wide mouth and a thin waist.** Many source-specific observations may
   enter; all durable facts, publications, scenes, exports, and derived imports pass through one
   exact store-resolved evidence/admission boundary.
4. **The companion is reconnaissance and fallback.** Direct Pump routes become primary one route
   at a time only after the existing Ember-present parity/promotion gate. Canonical chain truth
   comes from raw Solana evidence plus pinned decoders. The browser is not the production
   bloodstream.
5. **Forecasts are initially sealed.** Evidence, coverage, exact prices, descriptive fields, and
   earlier-only memory remain visible. Model probabilities and action rankings do not affect the
   live feed, alerts, sensing, presentation, or action affordances during the initial journal.
6. **One authority is extended, not duplicated.** SQLite/CAS, manifested Parquet, typed admission,
   and immutable derived artifacts remain the durable authority. Wave 5 does not create a second
   file registry, feature truth store, or model database.
7. **Realtime evidence does not imply realtime ML infrastructure.** Preserve incremental,
   timestamped interfaces. Initially run features, forecasts, adjudication, and evaluation on
   demand or in bounded jobs. No always-on model server or per-tick agent parliament is required.
8. **Research rigor is local, not paralyzing.** Missing executable-price evidence refuses only the
   claim that needs it. It does not hide observed social, wallet, lifecycle, chart, or stale-but-
   labeled information from the cockpit.
9. **Product evolution and experiments are distinct.** Normal-use Glass may evolve when Ember
   needs it. Confirmatory presentation claims require separately frozen, bounded, consented study
   windows. Every surface version remains a recorded intervention.
10. **Nothing in Wave 5 has economic authority.** A lane may observe, publish, retrieve, elicit,
    score, allocate a read budget, or advance a hypothetical branch. It cannot reserve household
    assets, build instructions, sign, submit, or install liquidity.

## 3. Independent qualification vector

Wave 5 has no scalar `complete` flag. Every witness reports attained capabilities, current health,
exact occurrence/build/contract digests, coverage and gaps, and disqualifiers. Capability sets are
partial orders rather than false universal ladders. For example, a scene can be replayable and its
release reproducible before its outcome matures; a presentation experiment need not pass through a
predictive stage.

| Track | Independently attained capabilities | Independent claim ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bloodstream | `fixture_walked`, `bounded_nonfixture`, `restart_recovered`, `sustained_observed` | evidence circulation only |
| Cockpit | `fixture_rendered`, `live_read_only`, `preliminary_ember_use`, `repeated_ember_use`, `critical_tasks_accessible` | assistive observation surface |
| Scientific memory | `scene_recorded`, `instrumented_choice_closed`, `outcome_matured`, `episode_replayable`, `release_reproducible` | point-in-time behavioral memory |
| Epistemic book | `claim_registered`, `forecast_before_issue_deadline`, `outcome_adjudicated`, `prospectively_scored`, `repeatedly_calibrated`, `operator_assist_admitted` | H3 forecast estimate only |
| Mechanics, per venue/profile | `exact_math`, `coherent_real_state`, `exact_quote`, `observed_simulation`, `observed_attempt`, `landed_fill_or_failure`, `whole_position_liquidation`, `terminal_position_closed` | named price/accounting objects only |
| Shadow policy, per policy | `retrospective_only`, `prospectively_frozen`, `prospectively_matured`, `repeated_supported` | H5 hypothetical proposal only |
| Research study | any locally valid subset of `fixture_recovered`, `descriptive`, `fitted_retrospective`, `prospective_shadow`, `admitted_presentation` | the registered study claim only |

Current health is a finite vector, not one mutually exclusive label. A subsystem may be healthy at
ingress while simultaneously backlogged at publication and gapped for one source. Components
include at least availability, freshness, backlog, coverage/gaps, refusal and resource pressure;
any summary severity has a deterministic, versioned precedence rule.

Attained maturity and current health remain separate: a source may retain
`sustained_observed` while presently unavailable. Tests cannot synthesize Ember-use capabilities;
they require an Ember-authored acknowledgment bound to the exact build/session and task.
“Critical tasks accessible” additionally requires keyboard, large-target, reduced-motion and
screen-reader task evidence; it is not inferred from unit tests alone.

## 4. Program topology

```text
                         +-----------------------------+
                         | W5-K known-truth/adversaries |
                         +---------------+-------------+
                                         |
W5-I bloodstream ignition --> W5-S sensorium/cockpit --> W5-M scientific memory
          |                         |                         |
          |                         |                         +--> W5-R earlier-only retrieval
          |                         |                         |
          |                         +-------------------------+--> W5-B epistemic book
          |
          +--> W5-C incremental mechanics capabilities ------+--> W5-P shadow policy

package-local prerequisites
  --> W5-L advanced learning/field incubator
```

The trunks mature independently. A failed model cannot demote a useful cockpit. A missing DLMM
fill cannot demote Pump social memory. A useful analog view cannot promote a forecast. A profitable
episode cannot promote presentation or policy claims.

Advanced packages do not share one global entrance. Descriptive topology requires memory plus
typed topology/units; presentation research requires scenes, consent and exposure evidence;
survival requires complete risk sets and censoring; response kernels require signed-flow coverage;
multimodal prediction requires matured outcomes and simple retrieval/baselines; coupled shadows
require named mechanics plus consolidated portfolio, reservation and memory closure.

## 6. W5-I — bloodstream ignition

The ignition track closes the Wave 4 operational joins using a registered dynamic census and
bounded hot-depth surface. It does not preselect one favorable historical mint.

### I0 — integration authority and run registration

Commit one typed run registration through the sole store before provider I/O. It contains the exact
canonical run/build/source-tree/config/budget/privacy bytes and digest. Export that immutable
registration to the collector; bind every census/hot `AttemptReservation`, control write and batch
policy to its occurrence ID and digest. A loose content-addressed file cannot qualify the run.

Reuse the existing receipt, catalog, publication, export, and derived-artifact contracts. Root
manifests, schema migrations, public routes, and readiness witnesses have one owner during
implementation. A dirty or unborn source tree may run only a foreground local canary bound to an
exact tree/build digest; no immutable remote release or service is claimed before an explicitly
authorized first revision.

Gate: changed same-ID bytes, mixed semantic/serialized digest domains, unknown fields, unresolved
references, future cutoffs, or authority widening refuse before side effects.

### I1 — real collector runtime

Add the absent foreground `joshi-collector run` surface with strict configuration, purpose-scoped
credential handles, source generations, reconnect/backoff, source-contract-specific progress
records, hard request/byte/credit budgets, bounded queues, and shutdown deadlines. A successful
progress result is captured evidence, a durably captured bounded-empty response whose source
contract authorizes an absence claim, or explicit gap/unavailable state. A quiet socket or generic
heartbeat never proves coverage.

The collector may read providers and append to its own spool. It may not write SQLite, call Glass,
read wallet material, execute analysis, or construct a transaction. Reservation precedes every
request or connection. Output reaches a fsynced spool segment or produces an explicit scoped gap.

### I2 — spool-to-catalog circulation and semantic admission

Build the private core orchestration:

```text
verify sealed segment
  -> atomically admit exact durable batch through sole SQLite writer
     (only this transaction may advance its durable cursor)
  -> receive exact store receipt
  -> construct and verify exact SpoolCatalogReceipt
  -> durably commit spool/catalog/batch/receipt closure
  -> collector fsyncs catalog ACK
  -> retain the exact origin segment throughout Ignition
```

Then build private semantic writers that parse exact bytes, recompute digests, resolve evidence,
coverage, finality, validity and availability from persisted rows, and only then construct
source-fact, publication, export-validation and restricted-import capabilities. Prospective choice
capabilities remain in M/B and are not an Ignition dependency. Public low-level constructors are
not proof.

A crash after batch commit but before catalog-receipt registration leaves a truthful admitted
cursor and ACK backlog; retry closes the remaining chain. Catalog or replica ACK never authorizes
deletion. Wave 4 has no deletion controller, so the exact origin segment remains retained.

Gate: killing the process after reservation, spool rename, store commit or receipt registration
yields an idempotent retry and never a false ACK. Publication/export crash gates belong to I4/I5;
I7 composes the full matrix.

### I3 — broad semantic surface

Store-resolve separate census/lifecycle, public wallet/transaction, acquisition-policy, hot
account/pool, product/social and coverage artifacts without collapsing their epistemic or
protection classes. Complete the W4 wallet-topology history registration/readback across restart.
An explicit point-in-time surface projection then binds:

- declared eligible universe and surface version;
- eligible count and digest complete within the declared surface/cutoff only when required coverage
  closes; otherwise the sample inclusion denominator and missing coverage;
- selected/rendered subset, omissions and ordering policy;
- hot, cold/control and denominator-only membership;
- source coverage, gaps, staleness, refusal and unknown states; and
- evidence and reducer cutoffs.

Full and incremental reduction agree at one cutoff. Later corrections do not enter older cuts. The
hot control path preserves `HotScopeIntent -> Desired -> reserved control_write -> Applied` or
`Degraded`; only then is hot acquisition reserved before I/O. `Applied` proves local collector
control state, not provider acceptance or coverage. A hot subject traces to committed census
evidence, the exact intent/lease/control receipts and its own pre-I/O acquisition reservation.

### I4 — immutable cockpit publication and paired read

Commit source-fact closure, projection, exact scene and broad cockpit publications through the
semantic store boundary. First decide and prove one of two schemas: Cockpit V1's exact manifest is
privately parsed and transitively closes the broad source-fact surface, or a minimal Cockpit V2 adds
a typed sorted source-fact reference list. Broad candidates may not hide inside opaque launch JSON.

Implement ordinary same-origin pairing as an actual one-time-code registry/exchange with expiry,
revocation and fault-tested non-secret session-occurrence/revocation metadata. One-time codes and
capability bytes remain memory-only, zeroized, never hashed/logged/stored, and restart invalidates
them. Ordinary pairing remains distinct from prospective launch-bound pairing.
Mount production immutable-publication routes. Prepare/commit/head crashes expose either the prior
complete publication or the new complete publication, never a mixture.

The publication is allowed to contain a bounded rendered subset, but it cannot omit the denominator
digest, policy, coverage or gaps. Private/authenticated source bytes never enter the public Glass
artifact. Exact-mint inspection is a child capability, not the root object.

### I5 — production research round-trip

Expand production Snapshot V2 mappings beyond scene-only fixtures to nonempty source occurrences,
coverage/gaps, census membership, hot-scope decisions, wallet/flow/market facts, publication and
provenance relations. Independently Rust-read and Python-validate the Parquet release. Run one
deliberately boring coverage/census diagnostic and import it under `descriptive_noncausal`.

Gate: exact values beyond `2^53` remain exact; altered, incomplete, unknown-contract or future-known
objects refuse; restart reads the same export and imported artifact by occurrence ID and content
digest; the source truth fingerprint is unchanged by analysis. Preserve distinct identities:
export-request occurrence is not content snapshot ID, analysis-run occurrence is not artifact ID,
and import occurrence is not artifact ID. Exact imported bytes live in verified CAS and survive
restart; a metadata row alone does not qualify. Unsupported prospective relations continue to
refuse rather than disappearing.

### I6 — durable operational status

Implement the absent supervisor/store/status adapters, query route and durable degradation/recovery
records. Receipt/cursor/gap/publication progress comes from durable subsystem state, not log
scraping. CPU, RSS, file-descriptor, disk and clock readings remain sampled operational observations
with explicit sample clocks unless separately appended; they are not silently durable truth.
Health can observe but cannot ACK, advance a cursor, publish, or promote readiness.

### I7 — ignition witnesses

Use three monotonic witnesses:

1. **Offline fault walk:** dynamic fake census with hot and cold subjects; crash every durability
   boundary through source → spool → catalog → fact → publication → export/import → status;
   backup; restore into a new path; verify before opening; reproduce all logical digests.
2. **One-hour nonfixture canary:** at least two eligible subjects, one dynamically promoted hot
   subject, one denominator-only cold subject, producer-derived applied/degraded watched-wallet
   receipts, two immutable cockpit cuts with advancing source/catalog cutoffs and explicit
   supersession, nonempty source/coverage/census/hot/provenance export/import and restart readback.
   Every enabled source has a source-contract-specific positive coverage proof or an explicit
   `unknown`, `unavailable` or gap. A quiet socket is not evidence of absence.
3. **Twenty-four-hour local soak:** test both cases. During a core outage the collector continues
   bounded spooling and later drains backlog exactly. During a collector outage acquisition stops,
   an explicit downtime gap opens, and a new generation resumes or boundedly backfills where the
   source permits. Retain reconnect generations, publication cadence/missed-publication causes and
   backup/restore/replay closure.

Profit, forecast skill, quote accuracy, an Ember choice and a prospective economic outcome are not
part of ignition qualification.

## 7. W5-A — live acquisition and provider policy

Source authority is field-specific:

| Material | Primary | Secondary or fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pump product membership/order, callouts, follows, community state | direct Pump route after route-specific promotion | companion reconnaissance, drift and bounded fallback |
| launch, trade, lifecycle, wallet and pool facts | raw Helius/Solana evidence plus pinned decoders | typed Pump/PumpPortal provider assertions |
| exact-mint product enrichment | Pump-documented `coins-v2/{mint}` as bounded mutable current enrichment | metadata URI/on-chain state; chain wins protocol/token-program conflicts |
| hot wallet and mint flow | compact Helius subscription plus finalized reconciliation | bounded secondary stream after capability review |
| pool state | direct Pump/PumpSwap/DLMM accounts | quarantined provider-decoded comparison |

An authenticated product failure means coverage is `unknown/unavailable`, not “no callouts.” Equal
facts from two providers remain distinct acquisitions. Processed events are provisional and later
finality/canonicality changes append corrections.

### A0 — source, cost and privacy registry

Extend the existing acquisition policy with source access class, credential authority, methods,
schema fingerprint, commitment/finality, billing unit, quota/reset, retry/gap semantics, protection
domain, retention and kill switch. Do not introduce another budget system.

The existing PumpPortal key is not admitted to the read-only collector: it carries Lightning-wallet
signing authority. Current zero-priced PumpPortal methods still use that wallet-bearing API key;
“zero-priced” is not “unauthenticated.” Credentialed PumpPortal is omitted from the read-only
`joshi-collector`. A future use requires a separately reviewed capability-bearing process/security
domain; risk-capping does not turn a signing key into a read-only credential. A genuinely
unauthenticated public surface may enter only if separately documented and typed as a provider
assertion. Wallet private-key dotfiles are outside Wave 5 acquisition. Every secret is owner-only,
purpose-scoped and excluded from arguments, logs, metrics, evidence, digests and error text.

Authenticated Pump/social and operator material is `app_private` and sealed before crossing a
machine. Raw screenshots and raw social content remain Mac-local initially; pure replicas receive
ciphertext and no decryption key. Continuous raw authenticated social capture is blocked until a
real tombstone/release/deletion controller exists—the current spool intentionally has no deletion
method.

Before enabling continuous authenticated social scopes, implement and fault-test that private
retention controller across origin spool, CAS, replicas and exported/derived references, with
tombstone/release/deletion occurrences and receipts that never impersonate coverage. Until then,
authenticated social scopes are `unavailable` for continuity or bounded Ember-present Mac-local
captures with explicit manual retention disposition.

### A1 — four acquisition strata

1. **Census:** launches, completion/migration, canonical pool creation and health; minimal IDs,
   lifecycle predicate, raw evidence closure and clocks. No full-program trade tape.
2. **Warm surface:** promoted Pump board/callout membership, one-time exact-mint enrichment,
   lifecycle revisions and an independently sampled chain-census control. Preserve membership/
   order/pagination.
3. **Leased hot lanes:** initially at most five mints, ten watched wallets and, only after the
   private retention controller closes, ten authenticated social scopes;
   exact transactions, accounts, local quotes, wallet topology, pool/route state and social refresh
   at a 60-second baseline unless a smaller registered episode cadence applies.
4. **Episode fidelity:** one to three subjects for a bounded operator episode; consequential quote
   snapshots, full relevant transaction hydration, witnessed scenes and flat-watch/re-entry.

Every lease has an eligible denominator, reason, TTL, desired/applied/ACK closure, costs and
coverage. Keep cold/control census and protected portfolio/manual scopes in reserved budgets outside
hot-scope competition. The current policy uses recency/stable identity for activating hot subjects;
it does not yet implement a general priority scheduler, and model proposals remain nonactivating.
A future priority policy requires a separate contract and tests.

### A2 — compact-census bakeoff

The measured raw Pump/PumpSwap Helius logs burst was approximately `2 MB/s`, with a naive daily
projection near `188 GB`; this is not the default census. If the paid plan permits it, compare a
minimal provider-filtered transaction subscription against a bounded reference:

```text
covered log/signature interval
  -> exact finalized transaction hydration
  -> pinned Pump/PumpSwap decode
  -> declared create/migration predicate
```

Logs alone prove only program mention, may truncate and include failed transactions, and are not
ground truth. Measure reference gaps as well as candidate recall, bytes, credits, latency and parser
yield. The bakeoff may conclude `unavailable` or `sample_only`; it must not substitute full raw logs.

### A3 — direct Pump route promotion

Promote one product route at a time using ordinary Ember-present paired acquisitions, exact request/
response/order/membership closure, and the existing promotion report. The companion remains paused
recon/fallback after promotion. Personalized/social route absence never blocks chain census.

### A4 — paid-data canary ladder

Every metered run freezes and enforces a separate run budget. The figures below are planning
ceilings, not permission for an uncapped run:

| Canary | Scope | Hard planning ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C0 | no-network integrated fake-source walk | no provider cost |
| C1 | one public wallet conformance page | 25 reads, `maxProviderCredits <= 250`, 64 MiB ingress/durable, 60 seconds |
| C2 | up to three compact-census windows plus at most 60 raw-log seconds total for the hydrated reference | 10k credits, 256 MiB ingress, 128 MiB durable, 8 MiB/s |
| C3 | up to six hours: census, five hot mints, ten wallets, up to ten retention-qualified social scopes/60-second baseline, one earned Pump surface | 25k credits, 512 MiB ingress, 256 MiB durable |
| C4 | 72-hour selective acquisition profile after measured projection | 100k Helius credits total, <2 GB ingress/day, 0.1–1 GB compressed durable/day |
| C5 | seven-day operational variation; thirty-day research support | separately reviewed after C4 |

Before metered I/O, inspect plan/reset/included credits/autoscaling through supported account
surfaces. If remaining allowance cannot be observed or autoscaling cannot be capped, omit that
phase. Every run terminates at the first request, byte, durable-byte, time or local-credit ceiling;
an incomplete intended window remains incomplete. Reserve bounded worst-case request/frame cost and
declare maximum in-flight overshoot. Refuse sources whose overrun cannot be bounded. Reconcile
provider-observed billing afterward; JOSHI's counter does not impersonate an invoice.

### A5 — host sequence

The Mac runs foreground session-bound canaries first; crash, sleep or power loss opens a gap. It is
not the availability claim.

`persvati` is the preferred zero-purchase collector only after a supported LTS, dedicated identity,
release, credential, lid/suspend/reboot, disk and network gates. `hbox` remains encrypted replica/
batch only after its OS, memory/swap and ZFS risks are accepted; it is never the sole copy. A small
Hetzner collector is considered only after local evidence shows unacceptable gaps or agent
contention. It remains outbound-only, carries no wallet key, and uses the identical spool protocol.

The Mac remains the sole initial catalog/Glass writer. While it is offline, `persvati` or Hetzner
may only append bounded exact spool segments; they mint no catalog ACK and advance no durable
catalog cursor, and must degrade/stop before the control reserve. `hbox` receives neither provider
credential nor decryption key. A replica ACK never authorizes deletion.

Source-specific recovery remains explicit: Helius WebSocket reconnect does not close a gap; only
durably admitted bounded HTTP recovery may mark it recovered or partial. PumpPortal live streams
have no documented replay cursor, so disconnect gaps are ordinarily unrecoverable. Pump HTTP
polling is interval-censored; one page, empty/null, `304`, or full-page/no-cursor does not prove feed
completion. Auth failure means product coverage unknown. Queue saturation stops the runner and the
supervisor persists its exit/downtime gap.

One-time `coins-v2` enrichment applies to warmed, cold-sample or hot members, not every launch in
the broad daily census.

## 8. W5-S — sensorium and daily cockpit

### S0 — parity and accessibility constitution

Maintain a versioned Ember-approved `DailyUseSurfaceProfile` containing every Pump/JOSHI surface
and task Ember actually uses, with exact profile ID/digest and critical/optional designation:

```text
launch/new | boards/trending | live activity | callouts/follows
watched-wallet activity | search/direct mint | social/media/replies
creator/community | lifecycle/migration/pools | position/exposure context
```

For each surface record route/source, personalization, ordering/pagination, cadence, fields/media,
coverage/freshness and status:

```text
promoted_continuous | public_chain_alternative_not_product_parity | companion_fallback
degraded | unavailable | absent_by_design
```

`public_chain_alternative_not_product_parity` may satisfy launch/trade/lifecycle/pool fact needs;
it cannot satisfy personalized membership/order, follows/callouts, social/media or rendered-
product parity. `absent_by_design` requires Ember's approval and reason and does not count as
parity.

Define keyboard, large-target, screen-reader and reduced-motion task flows. Every critical surface
has an independent maturity/health entry. Pump changes create a new profile version; they do not
silently narrow the critical set or degrade a previously claimed surface.

### S1 — daily cockpit

Ship four persistent contexts:

1. broad stable attention surface;
2. hot coin/workbench context;
3. episode/exposure rail including runner, flat-watch and re-entry;
4. replay/interview queue.

Add feed-capability truth, search/mint paste, no reorder beneath pointer/focus, pending update
acceptance, semantic keyboard paths, and a direct escape to external manual execution. Glass has no
wallet key, signer, transaction builder or submit path.

Record the full served board, ranking/presentation policy, omissions, viewport, focus and explicit
switch to Pump/external tools with a reason. A product-parity gap may be logged; it is not treated
as user failure.

### S2 — product success gates

One ordinary session may qualify `preliminary_ember_use`. `repeated_ember_use` requires several
ordinary Ember sessions in which the critical `DailyUseSurfaceProfile` tasks are supported;
switching away is limited to manual execution or a recorded parity gap. A partial surface may still
be useful, but it reports `partial_surface` and cannot claim broad product parity.

For every qualifying session Ember binds an exact build/session acknowledgment that the surface
reduced pain/navigation burden, preserved orientation and was worth reopening. Dwell, clicks,
trades and profit do not qualify it.

Normal product evolution remains allowed. A separately frozen study mode is required before
claiming that a layout, explanation or ranking caused improvement.

## 9. W5-M — scientific memory and retrieval

### M0 — scene, act and operator-assertion memory

The display references an already committed server scene/publication. Actual mount/render/viewport
is a separate presentation occurrence. Navigation/focus telemetry is best-effort and may open a
typed gap. Every operator act is retained immediately in the local durable queue even when scene or
presentation closure is missing; it then carries a typed gap and remains unqualified for scene-
bound research admission. A later adapter may admit it against the already committed scene while
preserving that presentation gap, or explicitly refuse research use. Ordinary product use and
time-sensitive acts never wait on a new network round trip. Capture failure cannot block a mark,
`zap_intent`, external-manual-action declaration or escape to an external tool.

The exact scene contains universe/order/omissions, semantic chart window/transform, raw social
references, market/quote/portfolio state, coverage/gaps and cutoffs. The presentation occurrence
separately records actual render, viewport, focus and control evidence when available.

Use stable coarse acts such as:

```text
notice | inspect | compare | mark | watch_flat | arm_shadow
declare_take_some | declare_keep_remainder | zap_intent
declare_reentry | declare_close | correct
```

Type acts as observational/attentional acts or evidence-only capital/action intentions. A
`zap_intent`, `declare_take_some`, `declare_reentry` or similar record is genuinely an operator
intention, but never a transaction construction/submission, landing, fill or economic effect. Keep
a separate optional scene-bound operator assertion: verbatim fragment or personal opaque token,
multi-valued disposition and `cannot_articulate_yet`. Its taxonomy may split, merge or retire
without rewriting the stable act. One-tap text, voice fragments and semantic chart anchors occur
after durable capture and never become modal. Skip and absence are normal. Corrections and ontology
changes append; they do not rewrite the source utterance.

### M1 — episode, outcome and interview closure

Reconcile public-wallet/manual effects into bounded episode paths with partial realization, runner
retention, full exit, flat watching, re-entry, typed unknown intervals and unresolved effects/lot
associations. Completeness is a measured property, never the default output. Preserve no-trade
attention episodes and explicit abstention only when an instrumented choice protocol defines it.

Replay is two-pass: collect an `outcome_hidden_reconstruction` while outcomes remain hidden; only
the immediate M0 assertion is contemporaneous. Then optionally reveal outcomes and collect a
separately marked retrospective interpretation. Session close,
outcome-at-horizon, knowledge deadline and interview disposition receive typed DTOs, semantic
writers, receipts and export relations rather than arbitrary contract strings.

### M2 — reproducible scientific cuts

Produce point-in-time decision, scene, episode, social-transition, route and liquidity releases
from the operational store. “Complete choice set” means complete within the exact instrumented
surface publication, not the entire market or every alternative Ember might have imagined.

Gate releases by eligible coverage time, event count, typed gaps, restarts, matured outcomes and
decision count. Thirty nearly empty days do not qualify; one valuable week with an honest outage is
not erased. Statistical sufficiency belongs to each registered question.

### R0 — earlier-only analog memory

Ship deterministic filters and nearest-neighbor retrieval before prediction. Every retrieval binds
a named feature/distance specification, point-in-time cutoff, decomposed similarity, missingness/
gap behavior and an explicit `none_analogous` result. Decision mode exposes only evidence available
at the earlier decision; retrospective outcome reveal is a separately logged intervention.

Every neighbor decision cutoff is strictly earlier than the query decision cutoff. Query and
neighbor features are as-known at their respective cutoffs. No future outcome, later ontology or
identity correction, outcome-derived feature, or later availability may affect indexing, distance,
ranking or tie-breaking.

Compare against plain manual filters using outcome-blinded recognition/usefulness judgments.
No vector database is required.

## 10. W5-B — epistemic position book

### B0 — claim/adjudication spine

Add semantic manifest families to the existing store for claim definition, occurrence, forecast
submission, adjudication version and score. An occurrence freezes the scene, subject, exact
instrumented universe, cutoff/as-of, evidence closure, coverage, horizon, target, censoring,
scoring and authority before target release.

The realtime seam is deliberately small:

```text
preregister ClaimDefinition/version
  -> witness eligible event
  -> resolve and freeze scene/evidence/universe/as-of closure
  -> atomically commit ClaimOccurrence before target window opens
  -> accept mutually blind submissions until explicit issue deadline
  -> close issue window
```

`issue_deadline`, target-window origin and maximum input availability are distinct timestamps. The
occurrence commit proves the entire frozen preimage; it cannot point to later-filled evidence rows.
For every submission:

```text
maximum_input_availability
  <= submission_input_cutoff
  <= occurrence_information_cutoff
  <= occurrence_commit
  <= submission_production_time
  <= issue_deadline
  <= target_window_origin
```

Initial comparable first-round submissions consume the occurrence-frozen evidence manifest. Any
later information state requires a prospectively defined revision or landmark occurrence.

Maturation is a bounded job:

```text
registered knowledge deadline reached
  -> inspect eligible outcome evidence and coverage available by that deadline
  -> materialize eligible outcome observation when closure exists
     or append source_loss/interval/conflicting/refused/unsupported state
  -> append score only when the registered scoring contract admits that adjudication
  -> update retrospective support/calibration
```

Original occurrences and submissions never mutate. Revisions, if later allowed, occur only at
prospectively named landmarks or evidence classes and disclose visible parents.

### B1 — initial claim families

1. **Spot competing risk:** for one witnessed nomination/inspection, exact standardized size,
   direction, route/quote profile, horizon and lifecycle cell. Freeze net executable target and
   drawdown thresholds, fees, quote freshness, observation cadence, event-order/tie rule and

   healthy-survival state occurs first. This claim requires the named W5-C quote capability.
2. **Liquidity survival:** freeze exact asset direction, size, route set, quote profile,

   remains above that predicate; “fresh” is not an adjudication rule. This can begin before landed-
   fill calibration matures when its named quote capability exists.
3. **Runner retain versus liquidate:** only when an exact runner lot and common terminal manifest
   exist; keep a directly scored competing-risk forecast separate from frozen branch-value replay.

LP and routed-liquidity claim definitions may be typed but remain disabled until schedule/state,
external/self-flow, inventory, full-liquidation and replay support close.

Ember may supply a qualitative disposition, coarse probability bin, pairwise judgment,
`cannot_articulate_yet`, or abstention. Only a properly elicited probability/distribution receives
a proper score.

### B2 — prospective journal and visibility

Collect Ember's submission when volunteered, a lifecycle/horizon base rate, and at most one or two
simple mechanics/analog baselines. Ember's voluntary first-round elicitation occurs before any
machine or base-rate reveal. Ember, base rate, models and agents are mutually blind during the
first round; all component submissions are sealed before ensemble construction. A revision names
every peer/ensemble forecast visible to its producer.

During the initial journal, forecasts cannot affect acquisition, retrieval, prompts, ranking,
notifications, hot leases, quote refresh, presentation, action affordances or operator attention.
Retrospective scorecards appear after admissible adjudication; censored/refused/conflicting/
unsupported cases remain visible and initially unscored rather than becoming failures or zero.

The first approximately twenty mature occurrences validate contract closure, censoring and burden;
they do not establish stable calibration or model skill.

### B3 — scorecards

After admissible adjudication, scorecards retain every censored/refused/conflicting/unsupported
denominator, collection burden, support and coverage. Components are scored separately against the
registered base rate. The first mature occurrences validate closure and ergonomics; learned weights
require earlier matured, embargoed and nonadjacent windows.

### B4 — shadow ensemble

Only identical claim definition/occurrence, horizon, outcome space and conditioning contracts may
aggregate. The first ensemble is fixed equal-weight, lineage-capped and shadow-only, built after all
eligible components are sealed **and** repeated chronological, outcome-embargoed, nonadjacent
prospective support exists for the claim family. Before then, retain and score components
separately without an ensemble artifact. Duplicated prompts/checkpoints do not increase effective
member count. Learned/calibrated weights require still-earlier matured, embargoed, nonadjacent
windows; component and ensemble forecasts continue to be scored separately.

### B5 — registered information allocation

Forecast-informed sensing begins only after matured B3 score/support evidence plus measured
acquisition/attention costs and coverage behavior, in a separately registered post-baseline epoch.
A distinct `SensingDecision` freezes complete census denominator, cold/manual/portfolio floors,
inclusion policy, read/attention budget, costs, TTL, assignments and authority. Before then, VOI is
exploratory/offline only. A score, disagreement, uncertainty or VOI estimate cannot directly alter
hot scope, quote cadence, provider spend, retrieval, prompts or presentation.

EVSI additionally requires a named attainable action set and declared utility. Otherwise report the
multidimensional information/resource frontier only. Preserve score/regret/persistence beside exact
capital/inventory-time, attention, latency, fees and risk. Ratios are secondary views, never the
canonical objective. Any causal information-value claim additionally requires registered
assignments or defensible known inclusion probabilities.

### B6 — operator visibility gate

A forecast becomes operator-visible only after repeated prospective calibration and incremental
shadow decision value survive simple baselines, negative controls, coverage/support review and a
separate presentation-usefulness/safety experiment. Evidence and exact descriptive truth remain
visible regardless of forecast status.

## 14. Sequencing

### Phase 0 — freeze and fault

Implement I0–I6 against fake dynamic census/hot/cold evidence. Close the entire fake source → spool
→ catalog → fact → publication → export/import → status path and every crash boundary before
provider I/O. In parallel implement the source/cost registry, parity/accessibility profile and claim
contract types.

Exit: full offline fault walk and new-path backup/restore are exact; no public low-level capability
can bypass semantic admission.

### Phase 1 — bounded nonfixture ignition

Run C1/C2 conformance/bakeoff canaries. Then run a distinct one-hour selective canary under its own
frozen budget, qualify one compact-census path or label it a sample, circulate one dynamic hot lease
and publish two advancing cockpit cuts. No remote deployment and no model inference are required.

Exit: one-hour nonfixture witness, measured costs/recall/gaps and restart readback.

### Phase 2 — daily instrument and memory

Build the broad Glass surface and scene/act/operator-assertion/episode writers. Extend the already-
operational export with those relations. Use JOSHI in ordinary sessions; record parity gaps and
external-tool switches. Complete the twenty-four-hour core-outage plus collector-outage soak.

Exit: `live_read_only`, at least one honest `preliminary_ember_use` acknowledgment, replay-
recognizable scene, reproducible nonempty research cut and truthful current health. Repeated-use and
broad-parity claims remain separate later capabilities.

### Phase 3 — memory usefulness and epistemic journal

Ship earlier-only analog retrieval. Open B0/B1 claims when their scene and outcome closure exists.
Keep forecasts sealed; mature and score them retrospectively.

Exit: analog retrieval beats plain filters on a registered outcome-blinded usefulness/recognition
measure; at least one real claim is adjudicated or honestly censored; collection burden is accepted.

### Phase 4 — sustained observation and mechanics growth

Run selective 72-hour then seven-day observation after measured budget approval. Add mechanics
profiles opportunistically and build per-study releases. Thirty days is a research-support horizon,
not a condition for Glass usefulness.

Exit: sustained coverage/gap/cost report, several replayable episodes or truthful scarcity, and
independent capability status for every enabled venue/profile.

### Phase 5 — advanced learning and shadow work

Admit only the research packages whose data, baseline, known-truth and support gates are met.
Presentation, active sensing and H5 shadow policies require their own prospective registrations.

Exploratory read-only field/graph/kernel/social/chart R&D may run in parallel as soon as its local
data and epistemic-type requirements exist. Only promotion, causal/predictive language and operator
presentation wait for the stricter prospective gates.

Exit is per study. No advanced candidate is required for the living instrument to remain successful.

## 16. Decision boundary

Wave 5 proceeds one independently useful maturity step at a time. If forecasting fails, keep the
sensorium, memory and retrieval. If retrieval fails, keep faithful scenes and operator vocabulary.
If a source fails, keep its gap and the remaining field-specific sources. If active sensing fails,
keep the broad census and human nomination. If a mechanics profile fails, refuse only its dependent
claims. If coupled control fails, keep exact inventory and terminal accounting.

The program becomes foolish only when it asks speculative machinery to justify the instrument or
lets a compelling fixture impersonate contact with the market. The living instrument reverses that
dependency: actual use and actual evidence feed the science, and every speculative layer must earn
its place without weakening the foundation.
