## 1. Executive decision

Wave 6 turns the living instrument into a **reflexive field lab**: a read/record/replay/propose
system that can study how market state, observation, product presentation, Ember's attention and
perception, manual action, and later learning alter one another.

The core loop is:

```text
declared market and source coverage
  -> point-in-time typed field bundle
  -> versioned Glass presentation
  -> witnessed viewport, attention, mark, assertion, or abstention
  -> independently reconciled manual effect or no-effect episode path
  -> matured outcome or typed censoring
  -> reproducible field/response/operator release
  -> machine research desk evidence packet
  -> human-reviewed hypothesis or discriminating observation proposal
  -> separately preregistered sensing/presentation/shadow campaign
  -> a new observation and product-policy epoch, never a rewritten old one
```

The program is not one market predictor, one pressure score, one imitation model of Ember, one
autonomous research agent, or one routed-liquidity controller. It contains independently maturing
instruments:

- a dynamic, typed field atlas;
- an observed-response atlas with exact mechanical motion separated from later response;
- an open operator-perceived-state and complementarity model;
- a machine research desk that inspects evidence and drafts falsifiable studies;
- prospective epistemic, active-sensing, presentation, and shadow-policy campaigns; and
- an optional routed-liquidity shadow over one consolidated inventory.

Every ambitious layer is removable. If fields add nothing, retain exact native observables. If the
operator model fails, retain scenes, utterances, and episodes. If forecasts fail, retain the
scorebook and censoring ledger. If active sensing fails, retain the broad census and manual
nomination. If routed shadow work fails, retain route, bin, inventory, and liquidation truth. A
failure may demote its own claim; it cannot demote a witnessed Wave 5 instrument.

## 2. Inherited constitution and authority ceiling

Wave 6 inherits these non-negotiable rules.

1. **The object is the composite process.** The observed universe, surface ordering, render,
   viewport, inspection, operator scene, episode, portfolio, coverage, and later policy version
   remain in the estimand. A selected coin or chart row cannot stand for the whole process.
2. **The state is stratified.** Settlement, venue, route, event flow, lifecycle, wallet, social,
   product surface, operator/portfolio, and coverage/knowledge strata retain distinct identities,
   units, clocks, topology, missingness, and authority.
3. **A field is an indexed typed measure.** It names domain, carrier, reference measure, unit,
   clock, observation map, topology epoch, cutoff, coverage, operator, falsifier, and useful
   residue. Physics vocabulary supplies no evidence.
4. **Exact mechanics precede analogy.** Atomic settlement and profile-bound Pump, PumpSwap, and
   DLMM transitions remain H0/H1. A smooth curve, generic graph, embedding, or fitted operator
   cannot override a venue-native refusal or landed effect.
5. **Observed response is not caused impact.** Deterministic protocol motion, subsequent observed
   response, conditional prediction, frozen-path replay, and causal effect are different objects.
6. **Knowledge time is executable.** Every as-known join selects evidence valid at the event and
   available by the cutoff. Later identity, topology, outcome, interview, or model knowledge never
   enters an earlier release.
7. **Unknown is not zero.** Source loss, gaps, conflicts, unsupported profiles, route refusal,
   interval censoring, missing modalities, and unarticulated operator state stay typed and visible.
8. **Presentation and sensing are interventions.** A new Glass, ranking, hot-scope rule, model
   panel, or prompt creates a new composite-policy epoch. It cannot be treated as a neutral view of
   stationary history.
9. **One household inventory underlies all books.** Spot episodes, runners, LP positions, routed
   edges, reservations, capital-time, and terminal liquidation share exact assets. Internal
   transfers, internal policy credits, and owned self-routed fees cannot create consolidated PnL.
10. **No Wave 6 artifact carries economic authority.** It may observe, retrieve, render, elicit,
    forecast, score, allocate a bounded read/attention budget, or advance a hypothetical branch.
    It cannot reserve assets, construct instructions, sign, submit, install liquidity, or execute
    an economic act.

The authority ladder remains:

| Rung | Wave 6 use | Ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H0 settlement | finalized atomic balances/effects and reconciliation | declared boundary fact |
| H1 protocol kinematics | exact profiled transitions, quotes, bins, withdrawal | deterministic result or refusal |
| H2 descriptive | fields, funnels, observed response, route and episode summaries | observation-policy-scoped description |
| H3 fitted | forecasts, response operators, score increments, sensing value | calibrated conditional estimate |
| H4 latent/abductive | operator state, actor/community, compatible mechanisms | equivalence class, never hidden fact |
| H5 policy | presentation, read allocation, or shadow branch proposal | hypothetical read/record/propose only |

There is no promotion from H3 to causality, H4 to identity, or H5 to live authority.

## 7. Lane specifications

### W6-0 — authority, registry, and campaign spine

Own the shared contracts in section 6, a strict H0-H5 claim-language validator, artifact DAG
validation, and append-only promotion/parking decisions. Require distinct occurrence IDs and
content digests, canonical bytes, unknown-field refusal, exact clock types, wide numeric domains,

**Gate/falsifier:** dependency cycles, mutable paths, missing digests, mixed clocks, unknown units,
future cutoffs, widened authority, or a self-authored promotion refuse before side effects.

### W6-K — known-truth and counterexample laboratory

Build estimator-specific small generators before one grand simulator. Every candidate gets:

1. an identifiable recovery case with predeclared tolerance;
2. an observational-equivalence case requiring a set, bound, or refusal;
3. a shortcut trap;
4. a future-availability/leakage attack;
5. a coverage birth/death or activity-dependent outage;
6. a topology/profile/identity change;
7. a unit/gauge/orientation and wide-atom case; and
8. a reflexive case in which sensing or presentation changes later observations.

Required shared adversaries include exact atom closure, Pump floor-plus-one, virtual/real reserve,
DLMM one-bin traversal/share rounding, migration splice, same-slot reorder, hot-scope coverage

**Falsifier:** good real-data fit never waives failed recovery, false identification, silent numeric
narrowing, or unjustified certainty. The estimator remains fixture-only or is rejected.

### W6-R — response and memory atlas

The first response product is an atlas, not a universal caller, wallet, coin, or resilience score.
Target separate response families:

```text
signed market event -> typed mark/marginal/size-quote/fill response
flow event -> quote capacity and liquidity edit response
callout/social/creator event -> trade/wallet/attention transition response
route/LP event -> canonical versus aggregate quote and route-loss response
presentation/sensing event -> viewport/inspect/act/correction response
operator act -> later operator transition and separately market outcome
```

For every family, fit in this order:

1. empirical/seasonal/state and shuffled/shifted controls;
2. deterministic protocol component and raw observed response;
3. descriptive nonparametric response with overlap/support;
4. simple Markov/DAR, Poisson/negative-binomial, empirical hazard or linear kernel baseline;
5. state/history-dependent challenger; and
6. only with sufficient support, marked Hawkes, TIM/HDIM-style, survival, graph, or multimodal
   candidates.

Report event and wall time, both directions, exact size grids, lifecycle, pre-state, quote age,
route, participant evidence, overlapping events, source health, and cold census. The existing
fixed-window arrival screen remains a screen; an observed recovery ratio remains an observed
ratio; neither may be renamed Hawkes or propagator resilience.

**Entry:** signed-flow/target coverage, exact pre-state, known-truth recovery/refusal, and
measurement error below the registered effect scale. Mechanics-dependent response requires the
named `W5-G5` capability.

**Promotion:** held-out likelihood/proper-score/calibration gain over the named simple baseline,
stable support across nonadjacent epochs, residual and negative-control checks, and operator value
that survives presentation measurement.

**Falsifiers:** deterministic curve motion explains the result; response changes sign under valid
price/state alignment; gain vanishes under participant/lifecycle/platform/coverage controls;
future-shift/unrelated-mint controls perform similarly; or the effect is below quote/clock/source
error. A causal label remains unavailable without a separate identification design.

### W6-O — operator model and complementarity laboratory

Model Ember's sparse perception as an open, multi-valued, partially latent state, not a class label
or imitation target:

```text
z_t = distribution over compatible coordinates given
      field history + memory + portfolio + choice set + presentation
```

Candidate coordinates include direction, urgency, horizon, fragility, stored potential,
compression kind, liquidity susceptibility, actor/attention configuration, inventory concern,
confidence, and unnamed components. The schema always permits overlap, correction, abstention and
`cannot_articulate_yet`.

Keep five questions separate:

1. Can replay reconstruct the exact evidence and source assertion?
2. Can a frozen model predict Ember's mark/act under the presentation actually seen?
3. Does Ember's contemporaneous observation improve a separately defined market forecast over
   machine-only evidence?
4. Do machine-only field components improve Ember's later judgment or correction under a safe
   presentation?
5. Does disagreement identify a useful discriminating observation, abstention, or interview?

Do not optimize agreement alone. A model can imitate presentation-induced behavior and be useless;
Ember can disagree and be wrong; both can share a missing source.

**Entry:** `W5-G2`; natural-use, burden, or UI-effect claims require `W5-G3`. Outcome-visible
interviews are held out from decision-time features.

**Falsifiers:** label performance disappears after scene/presentation controls; unnamed or
multi-valued states are forced into a closed taxonomy; later interviews leak outcomes; machine
agreement merely reproduces current UI order; operator input adds no prospective value; or capture
changes urgent behavior. Preserve source marks and reduce elicitation burden.

### W6-E — epistemic claims and information frontier

bankroll.

Initial claim families, opened independently, are:

- lifecycle/size-specific spot competing risk;

- response persistence at a registered lag and target object;
- operator mark/act prediction under a frozen presentation;

- information revealed by a presentation through consented assignment; and

Every first round is mutually blind. Components, Ember, base rate and simple model remain separate;
an ensemble is issued only after all eligible components seal. The first occurrences validate

strictly earlier support. Effective member count is lineage-aware.

Report the information/resource frontier rather than a scalar:

```text
proper-score increment | attainable replay-regret change | persistence
calibration | support/coverage | capital/inventory-time | attention
latency | friction | risk | unresolved exposure
```

**Falsifiers:** definition instability, differential censoring, incomplete choice denominator,
lineage duplication, baseline failure, calibration/support failure, score gain below measurement
error, or information that rarely changes a supported shadow decision. No score or disagreement
may directly change sensing, presentation, prompts, ranking, or action affordances.

### W6-D — machine research desk

The desk is a reproducible research workstation, not an autonomous scientist. It may:

- reproduce an earlier packet under its exact environment; and

It may not:

notebook.

**Falsifiers:** suggestions depend on hidden mutable state, cannot reproduce, repeatedly chase

### W6-P — product and presentation science

The normal product remains a broad accessible sensorium. The lab adds an optional field workbench:

  + native exact geometry and executable price stack
  + marked flow/intensity tracks
  + field/response atlas with semantic tables
  + operator episode and portfolio rail
  + evidence/claim/contradiction notebook

1. fixed random census slice;

3. operator watchlist/manual nomination;

`Intent -> Desired -> reserved control write -> Applied|Degraded -> reserved acquisition` chain.

## 8. Product and operator learning loop

The loop is append-only and intervention-aware:

```text
1. Observe
   store-resolved evidence + coverage + exact source/knowledge clocks

2. Publish
   immutable broad cockpit cut + exact field/response/claim references

3. Present
   planned policy -> receipt -> mount -> visibility/focus/control or typed gap

4. Elicit without blocking
   inspect | compare | mark | annotate | cannot_articulate | abstain
   | watch_flat | request_read | request_shadow | external_manual_escape

5. Reconcile independently
   operator intent != transaction; public-wallet/manual effects join later from landed truth

6. Close the episode/outcome
   outcome-hidden reconstruction -> outcome/censoring -> optional retrospective interpretation

7. Release and inspect
   point-in-time field/response/operator artifacts -> desk packet -> contradiction docket

8. Propose, review and register
   human accepts/rejects a new sensing/presentation/claim/shadow campaign

9. Begin a new epoch
   new campaign, source, model, Glass or policy version; old evidence remains immutable
```

Low-friction capture is mandatory. No questionnaire blocks urgent inspection or the escape to
external manual execution. Immediate act, later outcome-hidden reconstruction, and later
outcome-visible explanation remain three different evidence classes.

The Glass may expose machine/Ember disagreement only when each side's input closure, calibration,
support and presentation visibility are available. Disagreement can request more observation,
show alternatives, or support abstention. It cannot produce an action rank or urgency alarm by
itself.

## 10. Cross-program scientific protocol

Every study begins with the ten-item preamble from `JOSHI_THOUGHT`: object, estimand, observed
universe, clocks/cutoffs, acquisition, confounds/identifiability, falsifiers, transfer limits, UI
implication, and smallest informative prospective study.

### 10.1 Chronological partitioning

- Use rolling train, calibration and later evaluation intervals.
- Embargo by maximum target horizon plus source revision/ingestion delay.
- Purge overlapping episode, mint/family/territory, wallet/actor, route and topology paths where
  they share futures.
- Hold out at least one lifecycle/topology/product-policy transition or declare it unsupported.
- Freeze preprocessing, lag/size grids, feature selection, thresholds, calibration and ensemble
  weights before opening evaluation.
- Register exploratory multiplicity; a retrospective winner creates a new prospective campaign.

### 10.2 Baseline ladder

| Question | Required simple challengers |
| --- | --- |
| field usefulness | exact native table, net flow, concentration, capacity, cycle count |
| event/flow response | seasonal/state baseline, shuffle/shift, Markov/DAR, cold census |
| survival/transition | empirical hazard, Kaplan–Meier/Aalen–Johansen, simple additive/Cox model |
| operator model | scene filters, surface order, prior mark, base rate, `cannot_articulate` |
| source/model information | same model without input, provider/renderer/time controls |
| active sensing | random, lifecycle/coverage strata, operator watchlist, cheapest source |
| presentation | existing Glass order and semantic table/text-only arm with common safety strip |
| routed shadow | no edge, matched-capital CPMM, uniform bins, unchanged schedule, do nothing |

### 10.3 Program-wide hard falsifiers

Stop or demote the affected claim when:

- future availability, later topology/identity, outcomes, interview or evaluation-trained encoder
  enters an as-known artifact;
- a field mixes units, carriers, topology epochs, authority rungs, or narrows wide atoms;
- a result disappears under a simple baseline, future-shift, unrelated-mint, cold-scope,
  provider/renderer, or lifecycle control;
- measurement, quote/fill, clock, route, coverage or censoring uncertainty is as large as the
  effect;
- support is confined to selected/high-coverage/surviving cases or one pair/regime;
- calibration fails in the exact displayed size/direction/lifecycle stratum;
- a latent equivalence class is rendered as identity or mechanism;
- presentation changes labels/actions more than it improves registered usefulness;
- economic advantage disappears after full costs, capacity, self-flow elimination, residuals and
  common terminal liquidation; or
- the claim needs the observed future held fixed after a changed action.

Apparatus failures produce `aborted_apparatus`, not a favorable or unfavorable hypothesis result.

## 13. What Wave 6 deliberately does not build

  signer, submitter, rebroadcaster, wallet connector or capital allocator;

  count treated as evidence;

  GPU platform or per-tick agent parliament;

## 14. Decision boundary

Wave 6 should begin only in its pre-witness, fixture-capped form while Wave 5 is still closing its
operational witness. Nonfixture field work begins after `W5-G1`; operator and episode science after
`W5-G2`; natural-use/presentation work after `W5-G3`; durable claim journaling after `W5-G4a`;
epistemic ensemble or claim-informed work after `W5-G4b`; mechanics-dependent work profile by
profile under `W5-G5`; and routed joint shadow only after `W5-G6`.

The Reflexive Field Lab earns its name when it can make the observation policy, Glass policy,
operator response, manual effect or no-effect, source coverage, and later research revision part of one
replayable causal-memory object while still refusing claims the evidence cannot identify. It does
not earn it by fitting a complicated field equation or producing a favorable shadow result.

The program succeeds asymmetrically. A negative field result can leave exact flow and topology
glass. A negative response result can leave a calibrated null and event atlas. A failed operator
model can leave a faithful open vocabulary. A failed active-sensing policy can leave a broad
census. A failed routed edge can leave exact inventory and route truth. A failed formalization can
leave adversarial fixtures. That durable residue—not autonomous execution and not a promise of
profit—is the reason to build Wave 6.
