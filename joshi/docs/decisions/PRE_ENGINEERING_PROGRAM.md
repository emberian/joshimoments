# Pre-engineering program

Status: proposed execution sequence; no engineering or live-capital authorization.

## Immediate authorization boundary

Authorize only:

1. the Pump surface/access feasibility spike below;
2. selection of the initial portfolio domain and one natural operator loop;
3. the semantic/adversarial design needed for Slice 1.

Do not yet authorize a full collector stack, application implementation, database selection,
transaction builder, signer, LP executor, automated watch policy, or paid infrastructure purchase.
The next decision checkpoint should choose an operating mode and approve a deliberately small
Slice 1 engineering plan.

## Why this sequence

The project has two independent premise risks:

- **product premise:** can Joshi lawfully and reliably preserve enough of the information surface
  Ember actually uses to become a natural cockpit rather than a post-selection logger?
- **economic-resolution premise:** can executable quote, latency, missingness, and accounting error
  be made small enough to study the very small gains a crackle seeks?

Building a platform before resolving the first risk repeats the original projection error at UI
scale. Building a paper engine before resolving the second repeats the old mark-versus-fill error.

## Spike 0 — one-loop truth test

### Observe before choosing the surface

During three ordinary sessions, record app-scoped notes about the current Pump/Padre loop with
Ember's explicit participation:

- which surface produced each material candidate;
- exact sort, filter, search, visible neighbors, rank movement, and update cadence;
- which images, badges, threads, replies, callouts, communities, identities, holders, flows, chart
  behaviors, and social relationships affected inspection;
- which transitions caused a switch between Pump, Padre, wallet, and other tools;
- how partial realization, full exit, flat watching, re-entry, and runner retention occurred;
- which cues were materially useful but not exposed by candidate sources.

Do not assume `New`, `Trending`, `Live`, or `Callouts` should be first. Choose the initial discovery
surface from observed use. App-scoped capture excludes unrelated windows, ambient keystrokes,
microphone, and continuous desktop video.

### Probe only that loop

For the selected discovery route and coin page, compare:

- candidate membership, order, filters, pagination, personalization, and latency;
- current Pump social/thread/community access under a documented lawful/operational basis;
- public on-chain launch, trade, reserve, migration, fee, wallet, and pool state;
- a genuine event-backed chart path across bonding-curve and migrated venues;
- query-only direct SDK and Jupiter quotes at sizes Ember would actually contemplate;
- read-only wallet history, balances, and current liquidation for the proposed initial portfolio
  domain.

Classify every relevant field as:

```text
public chain | authorized social/public | Pump-only | derived
mutable/current-only | delayed | unavailable | access unresolved
```

The spike also runs two bounded apparatus probes:

- a short two-source chain-stream test with deliberate disconnect/recovery and finalized backfill;
- one offline crash/replay fixture containing duplicates, repeated equal fills, source conflict,
  clock skew, parser drift, a gap, external exit, flat watch, re-entry, partial reduction, and a
  runner assertion.

These characterize feasibility; they are not a commitment to retain the chosen sources or store.

### Output and operating-mode decision

Produce:

1. a workflow trace for each session;
2. field/coverage/latency/access matrix;
3. sample witnessed scene and known omissions;
4. chart/quote lifecycle coverage report;
5. stream recovery and event-identity report;
6. initial portfolio-domain proposal with known-basis gaps;
7. one of four operating modes.

| Mode | Meaning | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **replacement-capable** | The selected Pump loop can be reproduced with stable enough access and measured fidelity. | Build one native discovery-to-workbench loop. |
| **companion-capable** | Direct sources are incomplete, but reviewed app-scoped capture of the actual Pump scene is permissible and reliable. | Keep Pump as renderer; Joshi adds episode, evidence, quote, accounting, and replay glass. |
| **on-chain observatory** | Neither parity nor faithful companion capture is available. | Define Joshi's own universe; do not claim to measure Pump selection. |
| **stop/rethink** | Inaccessible Pump-only texture is central and the reduced product would not earn natural use. | Pause replacement; retain only independently useful accounting/replay work. |

### Replacement-capable gate

For the chosen workflow, require:

- exact mint/order comparison for at least 100 consecutive sampled cards or list entries;
- at least 95% captured within the measured reaction window, with every mismatch retained;
- every cue Ember calls material reproduced, visibly unavailable, or deliberately left in a
  companion pane;
- genuine chart state and size-specific query-only quotes for at least 90% of inspected mints,
  with unsupported lifecycle/venue cases named;
- documented access basis, rate behavior, cache/mutability, authentication expiry, and breakage
  mode;
- a reconstructed scene Ember recognizes with no unknown material omission.

These are product-feasibility thresholds, not statistical evidence. Opaque personalization selects
companion or observatory mode rather than being rounded into parity.

## Slice 1 — exposure truth and one prospective episode notebook

This is the first engineering candidate after Spike 0, not current authorization.

### Operator value

One trustworthy place to see current exposure, understand the three named retained runners, mark
the next coin that matters, and replay one prospective episode while execution remains in Pump or
Padre.

### Minimal scope

- one declared read-only wallet, widened only when actual internal transfers make that boundary
  false;
- RADON, EarthCoin, and CRASHIUS reconstructed as current accounting/exposure fixtures;
- exact quantities, basis quality, realized proceeds, and full-size executable liquidation or
  honest unquotability;
- one exact-mint workbench with an event-backed chart and explicit source health;
- one persistent episode/exposure rail;
- acts for mark, watch, shadow arm, partial-intent, keep-remainder, exit-intent, watch-flat,
  re-entry-intent, and resolution, with optional free text or `not articulable`;
- append-oriented operator records and a small scene manifest at consequential acts;
- read-only observation/reconciliation of external wallet changes;
- one witnessed/outcome-hidden replay followed by a separate retrospective annotation.

The old runner histories test accounting only. The next naturally encountered episode is the
first valid prospective scene. Do not trade to manufacture a fixture.

### Gate before Slice 2

- exact quantity reconciliation or a named unresolved cause for every residual;
- runner view does not call retained exposure free and does not fabricate missing basis;
- one natural external partial/full action is detected without inventing intent or fill time;
- an episode can survive exact flat and link a later re-entry to a new inventory epoch;
- witnessed replay restores chart, quote, exposure, gesture, product version, and known gaps;
- Ember finds the surface useful for management or memory rather than clerical bookkeeping;
- no key, builder, signer, broadcast client, or transaction endpoint exists.

If this feels like bookkeeping that Ember avoids, revise or stop before adding feeds.

## Slice 2 — one discovery surface and replayable workbench

### Operator value

Perform one common discovery-to-inspection loop in Joshi without losing material context, while
all economic action remains external.

### Addition

- the single discovery surface selected by Spike 0;
- served order, rendered set, viewport, list-freeze/target stability, and navigation provenance;
- chart, market, flow, social, thread, and identity cues proven material in Spike 0;
- external-execution links by exact mint and later wallet reconciliation;
- scene state plus app-only screenshot around consequential gestures.

### Gate before Slice 3

- used as the primary observation surface for at least five ordinary sessions, including two
  consecutive sessions after the last material UI change;
- leaving for manual execution is expected; leaving to recover missing discovery or context is a
  recorded parity defect;
- at least 95% of gestures replay recognizably without manual repair;
- every gesture names choice context, viewport, chart domain, source watermarks, quote/portfolio
  snapshot, and product version or is visibly incomplete;
- every wallet delta reconciles or remains unattributed; no fabricated pre-action scene;
- gesture overhead remains below an Ember-chosen threshold and no urgent exit/re-entry is missed
  because annotation intervened.

Do not add more feeds when this gate fails. Repair the loop or explicitly select companion mode.

## Slice 3 — shadow crackle and episode-management instrument

### Operator value

After Ember selects and arms a coin, Joshi supplies high-resolution vigilance, exact net hurdles,
and counterfactual memory without capital authority.

### Addition

- `ARM SHADOW CRACKLE` with exact intended size, expiry, and observe/shadow effect ceiling;
- a mint hot scope continuing through flat watching when the episode remains live;
- current direct/Jupiter query-only buy and sell quotes bound to venue, effective reserves,
  dynamic fees, size, network/account assumptions, and clocks;
- several explicitly provisional microdip interpretations in parallel;
- latency-perturbed hypothetical paths, never “shadow fills”;
- no-entry/expiry, immediate-entry, partial, retain, full-exit, flat, re-entry, and hold-through
  comparisons at common horizons;
- a small comparison set drawn from actual viewed/rejected candidates;
- optional immediate fragments and outcome-separated replay interviews.

### Apparatus gate

Run until at least 20 naturally armed episodes or two weeks of ordinary use, whichever is later.
This is not a profitability sample-size claim.

Require:

- every counterfactual recomputable from exact source state or labeled unquotable;
- quote/replay uncertainty materially smaller than the target: p95 discrepancy or latency envelope
  below 20% of the smallest net-profit hurdle Ember would actually act on;
- selection, waiting, execution, partial realization, runner transition, full exit, flat watching,
  re-entry, and episode resolution inspectable separately;
- Slice 2 natural-use and burden gates remain satisfied;
- a coverage/latency/accounting report and frozen later evaluation protocol, not an EV verdict.

If ordinary apparatus error can reverse the tiny target, that target is unresolved regardless of
how attractive its marks look.

## Slice 4 — earn breadth one lane at a time

Select the next addition from observed unmet needs. Candidates include another Pump surface,
fancoin transition context, followed-wallet routing, territory/family strip, consolidated runner
glass, or read-only LP schedule visualization.

Every candidate begins as a pane or query over the shared tape. It does not receive a separate
warehouse, graph database, entity platform, scoring engine, or automation path.

### Followed-wallet pilot

If normal use confirms followed accounts are materially part of Ember's sensorium:

- start with at most 10–20 explicitly promoted profiles for 14 days;
- distinguish Pump follow, profile/author wallet, directly signed trade wallet, funder, relayer,
  and inferred coordination;
- use only decoded direct signed trades for candidate alerts;
- record every side/partial/return, per-wallet source health, chain reconciliation, receipt
  latency, current quotes, anonymous-flow controls, later board/callout time, and operator response;
- alert once per mint/scene, show evidence rather than a “smart wallet” badge, and never auto-copy.

Continue only if at least one verified account repeatedly adds lead time or selection context
beyond anonymous flow at tolerable attention cost. Shelve it as a strategy input if it arrives no
earlier, identity adds no held-out information, or it mainly induces FOMO.

### Territory pilot

For attended, fancoin, or wallet-promoted candidates only:

- keep at most 20 hot territories for 14 days;
- seed exact metadata/image/deployer family proposals without declaring them truth;
- show exact mints, launch times, relationship evidence, community/flow/liquidity shares, competing
  alternatives, and a provisional leader only by a named metric;
- preserve launch resemblance, community overlap, directed capital migration, and inferred fleet
  relations as different edge types;
- allow `same`, `not same`, `OG for now`, `moving to`, free text, or no articulation.

Continue only if the strip reveals meaningful rivals, predecessor/successor relations,
fragmentation, or movement that Ember would otherwise miss, or adds supported information beyond
coin-local state. Shelve the higher-order layer if it mostly produces collisions/farms, migration
collapses to ordinary wallet rotation, or maintenance costs more attention than it returns.

### General breadth gate

- used in at least three ordinary sessions;
- adds a decision, comparison, avoided error, new inspection, or explicit rejection;
- evidence and point-in-time missingness survive replay;
- uncertainty is presented rather than hidden behind identity or model confidence;
- does not displace the primary loop with noise.

“A future model may want it” is not enough to keep a lane.

- the evaluation protocol names population, strategy/UI version, action/policy, economic outcome,

## First evaluation claims

The diary should eventually support distinct protocols for:

- operator selection uplift across adjacent funnel stages;
- value of waiting versus immediate entry/no entry;
- actual exit–flat–re-entry path versus hold-through and sell-only;
- partial realization and runner retention versus sell-none/sell-all under a declared objective;
- value per attention-hour under concurrent shadow baselines;
- scale decay from Ember's ranked top choices to machine-nominated breadth;
- social/fancoin transition warning and calibration;
- wallet-hit incremental discovery beyond anonymous flow;
- LP schedule management versus declared passive/hold alternatives.

Failure of one component does not settle another. A null is “unresolved” when apparatus quality or
support is inadequate and a real component finding when the prospective protocol clears those
gates.

## Stop/rethink conditions

Pause or narrow the project when any of these persist after a bounded repair attempt:

- Pump-only information central to Ember's decisions cannot be lawfully/stably observed or captured,
  and an on-chain-only surface does not earn natural use;
- Ember repeatedly returns to other products for context rather than only execution;
- operator capture becomes clerical work, changes the policy materially, or misses urgent acts;
- source gaps, identity drift, or future enrichment prevent recognizable point-in-time replay;
- wallet quantities and external effects cannot reconcile under the declared portfolio domain;
- executable quote/latency uncertainty is of the same order as every interesting crackle target;
- the work accumulates schemas, collectors, labels, or models faster than prospective episodes;
- financial pressure is being used to justify widening loss, authority, or evidence standards;
- a strategy claim is repeatedly redefined after outcomes to avoid its falsifier;
- later live architecture cannot structurally isolate keys, durable reservations, transaction
  validation, and unresolved-order reconciliation.

These conditions do not necessarily invalidate the accounting, replay, or research apparatus. They
may select a smaller terminal product: an exposure manager, Pump companion, or on-chain observatory.
