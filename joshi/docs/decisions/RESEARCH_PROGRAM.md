# Research program

Status: integrated research posture; begins only after the apparatus gates in
`PRE_ENGINEERING_PROGRAM.md`.

## Claims must remain separate

| Level | Claim | Evidence that could support it |
|---|---|---|
| **instrument validity** | Joshi preserves what was available, rendered, intended, quoted, executed, owned, and later learned. | deterministic replay, balance closure, coverage, no time travel, quote/fill calibration |
| **workstation value** | The glass improves perception, reaction, accounting, memory, comparison, or containment. | natural use, fewer context switches/errors, useful replay, low capture burden |
| **composite-policy value** | Ember's prospective selection, timing, and management beat an attainable alternative net of costs and opportunity. | sealed multi-regime episode comparison with exact execution/economic boundaries |
| **component value** | One watcher, retrieval aid, transition model, or synthesized rule adds value without destroying the rest of the process. | supported chronological evaluation against the prior process version |
| **scale value** | Marginal candidates remain useful as breadth, capital, turnover, and autonomy increase. | explicit scale ladder with tails, capacity, impact, and prior rung retained |

Evidence for a lower level does not establish a higher one. A beautiful replay does not prove
alpha; a narrow profitable selection does not prove market-wide automation. Conversely, every
economic family may fail while the workstation remains worth using.

## Versioned hypothesis registry

Every hypothesis or exploratory analysis receives:

- claim level and strategy/component family;
- originating scene, utterance, interview, result, or external theory;
- eligible universe and attention-stage denominator;
- policy, UI, source, ontology, and accounting versions;
- action/path being compared;
- intended size, horizon/terminal rule, fees, quote/latency/fill assumptions;
- dependency unit and regime definition;
- missingness, disappearance, and capacity treatment;
- exploration status, promotion criteria, stopping rule, and falsifier;
- every attempted transformation/model/result, including failures.

Exploration can remain playful and high-dimensional. A confirmatory claim freezes the fields above
before a future chronological block is opened. Reusing that block makes it exploratory evidence
for the next version.

## Primary estimands

### Attention and selection

Estimate adjacent funnel contrasts under a common downstream shadow policy:

```text
census eligible -> surfaced -> rendered -> viewport -> opened
                -> inspected/rejected -> armed -> triggered -> externally traded
```

The main question is whether Ember's selections rank opportunities relative to contemporaneous
alternatives, not whether looking at a coin causally changes its return. Preserve unordered
shortlists, pairwise preferences, or “none” rather than inventing a total rank from click time.

### Entry timing

For a prospective arm, compare at actual contemplated size:

- immediate entry;
- each predeclared microdip interpretation;
- actual external entry if any;
- no entry/expiry;
- latency and adverse-fill perturbations.

Report both a common-calendar path and a fully restarted fixed management policy. Do not hold later
discretionary exits constant and call the difference a causal value of waiting.

### Exit, flat watching, and re-entry

For one episode and common terminal horizons, compare:

- actual sell–flat–rebuy path;
- uninterrupted hold;
- sell and remain flat;
- simple predeclared management alternatives.

Preserve additional friction, avoided downside, forgone upside, cash released, attention cost,
current exposure, and downstream opportunity separately. A lower new basis never erases the loss
before re-entry.

### Partial realization and runner retention

At a partial decision, compare sell-none, actual fraction, and sell-all at the same executable
state with a frozen future rule. Separately evaluate the complete adaptive runner book. Preserve:

- proceeds and risk removed;
- remaining basis and liquidation value;
- retained tail payoff;
- recycling opportunity;
- correlated runner exposure and cemetery cost.

One giant winner cannot validate the policy, and median return cannot by itself refute a convex
tail policy. The declared book objective and conservative liquidation bounds decide the relevant
comparison.

### Attention value and scale decay

Measure active attention, elapsed session time, interruption burden, opportunities inspected, and
the incremental value of assisted versus concurrent shadow baselines. Do not infer an hourly wage
by comparing attended profitable hours with unattended missing data.

Scale is a curve over rank/breadth, capital, concurrency, impact, tail correlation, feedback delay,
and human review—not hand-selected value multiplied by market size. Human top choices, machine-
nominated candidates, and autonomous coverage are different policies.

### Social, wallet, ecology, and LP

- transition timing and calibration under point-in-time identity/community evidence and competing
  coin families;
- followed-wallet incremental discovery and lead time beyond anonymous flow and boards;
- territory membership quality, rival discovery, leader switch/fragmentation, and directed
  migration relative to collision/rotation baselines;
- LP fees, composition, active schedule, rebalance/removal friction, and opportunity value relative
  to passive LP and hold benchmarks.

None begins with a scalar sentiment, smart-wallet, territory, or yield score.

## Methods in an honest order

1. **Prospective census and hot scopes.** Establish denominators, source coverage, and high-
   resolution attended scenes.
2. **Descriptive episode and portfolio accounting.** Exact actual path before explanation.
3. **Matched choice sets and stable shadow fan-out.** Use contemporaneous alternatives and a small
   fixed family of attainable actions.
4. **Multi-state and competing-risk descriptions.** Board disappearance, route loss, migration,
   social transition, flat watching, re-entry, fragmentation, and death are outcomes, not ordinary
   censoring.
5. **Analog retrieval.** Retrieve causally prior chart/flow/social/portfolio/operator scenes and
   show heterogeneous paths; collect `same`, `different`, and `none analogous` labels.
6. **Multimodal and transition models.** Separate representation, gesture imitation, transition
   prediction, fixed-policy outcome estimation, and attention ranking.
7. **Counterexample-guided synthesis.** Synthesize advisory rules from positive examples,
   counterexamples, temporal constraints, unknowns, and safety-independent partial specifications;
   replay disagreements and test the next version chronologically.
8. **Hybrid-system/circuit abduction.** Use continuous state, discrete modes, changing identity/
   community topology, external inputs, and controller actions to discover missing sensors and
   equivalence classes of mechanisms—not a unique stationary market circuit.

System-randomized interface or shadow probes can have known propensities. Human choices generally
do not. OPE is admissible only with conditional support, known behavior probabilities, observed
trajectory rewards, and adequate effective sample size.

## Strategy-family falsifiers

### Crackle

Park the measured crackle component when sealed prospective arms show no advantage over immediate
entry or no entry after executable costs, or when normal quote/latency/fill uncertainty overwhelms
the target and tails violate the declared loss envelope. A mechanical whole-market null does not
falsify an unobserved operator-selected policy.

### Active exit/re-entry management

Park the measured cycling component when total episode utility repeatedly fails to beat attainable
hold, fixed-management, and sell-only paths at common horizons, with endpoint selection, all
friction, missed upside, and opportunity cost controlled.

### Runners

Park discretionary retention when a prospectively budgeted book underperforms full liquidation or
a simple fixed residual rule under conservative liquidation, or when correlated cemetery risk is
unacceptable. Preserve the possibility that the useful rule is narrower than discretionary
promotion.

### Fancoin/social transition

Park the economic claim when identity/transition reconstruction has unacceptable false linkage,
prospective ranking adds no information beyond simple flow/age/liquidity baselines, evidence arrives
only after price, duplicates make the target unstable, or useful lead time lacks executable
capacity.

### Wallet routing and territories

Shelve wallet identity as a strategy input when it adds no lead time or held-out information beyond
anonymous flow, or mainly induces FOMO. Shelve higher-order ecology when families are mostly
collisions/farms, migration becomes ordinary rotation, or the context adds neither operator value
nor supported information. Retain the underlying evidence in the shared scene when independently
useful.

### LP management

Park active LP policy when fees net of adverse composition, rebalance/removal friction, downtime,
and opportunity cost remain inferior to declared passive/hold baselines, or intended risk cannot
be controlled without swaps the capability forbids. Exact schedule visualization may remain a
valuable treasury tool.

## Adversarial study suite

Every promoted analysis faces relevant members of this suite:

- prospective scene freeze and time-travel canaries;
- adjacent-stage matched risk sets;
- stable shadow policy fan-out;
- measured latency/adverse-fill break-even attack;
- disappearance, unquotability, and zero-recovery bounds;
- blind/outcome-hidden and later outcome-aware elicitation;
- known-zero worlds and planted effects preserving real dependence;
- timestamp, entity, fee, direction, censor, and book-transfer mutation;
- sealed chronological confirmation;
- scale ladder with the previous rung retained;
- clustered crisis/common-cause replay;
- reversible UI counterfactuals for badges, alerts, colors, rankings, and recommendations.

If ordinary apparatus uncertainty reverses a result, label it execution-fragile or unresolved—not
positive. If a planted relevant effect cannot be recovered, a null is not informative.

## Reporting contract

Every economic report includes:

- actual and effective sample units by episode, mint, family/community, session, and regime;
- prospective versus retrospective labels;
- complete positions plus conservative bounds for unquotable assets;
- chart marks, executable quotes, hypothetical paths, transactions, and reconciled fills in
  separate types;
- SOL-native wealth, dated USD view, capital-time, turnover, attention, drawdown, tail bounds,
  capacity, and remaining exposure;
- current source coverage/gaps and model/parser/UI/policy versions;
- unsupported regions and `not identifiable` where conditions fail;
- results at the claim level actually tested.

Positive PnL is not an apparatus invariant. Frequent wins, closed-trade averages, and “original
capital recovered” are never sufficient book metrics.

## Progress worth recognizing before profit

- exact wallet and LP custody closure;
- recognizable witnessed replay without future enrichment;
- calibrated quote/latency/disappearance envelopes;
- a natural low-distortion cockpit loop;
- fewer accidental exposures, forgotten runners, or misunderstood LP actions;
- a stable distinction recovered from prospective gestures;
- a plausible component killed cleanly;
- a model that retrieves useful analogs or predicts a transition on unseen chronological scenes;
- tested aggregate loss, stale-source, and future capability boundaries.

These results justify further investigation, not live authority or a profitability claim.
